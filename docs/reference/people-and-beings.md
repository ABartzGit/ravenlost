# People & Beings

A quick-reference list of named people and beings Ravenlost has encountered or heard about.

| **Name** | **Type** | **First encountered** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Henry Loust** | Spirit | Black Lantern Inn | Ravenlost's original employer. Initially hired the party on behalf of Lady Estella Godfroy. Later encountered in Candle Cross and gave Bia a letter granting Ravenlost access to the House on Griffin Hill to meet Lord Godfroy. |
| **Lady Estella Godfroy** | Human / patroness | Black Lantern Inn | Henry Loust's patroness and a member of the Mordent Godfroy family. Wanted Glim Brightstone's journal recovered. |
| **Glim Brightstone** | Dwarf alchemist | Glim Brightstone's laboratory | Disappeared about 65 years ago. Developed an apparatus that could chart a path through the Mists and potentially interfere with a dreadlord's control. A clockwork ballerina at the DeLawrence Pouchenelle manor suggests someone in that family knew him personally. |
| **Cedgewick** | Automaton | Glim Brightstone's laboratory | Butler automaton created by Glim. Stored Glim's journal entries on wax recording tubes. Portions of his memory were deliberately erased. |
| **Victor Mordenheim** | Dreadlord | Glim Brightstone's laboratory | Dreadlord of Lemordia. Cedgewick identified the toe-walking creatures that attacked the laboratory as Victor's work. |
| **Wilfred Godfroy** | Dreadlord | Glim Brightstone's laboratory recordings | Dreadlord of Mordent. Glim's final known course led toward his domain. |
| **Madam Eva** | Fortune teller | Black Lantern Inn | Appeared after the inn revealed itself to be abandoned. Gave Ravenlost a card reading that may refer to people they know or will encounter. |
| **Parry** | Traveling merchant | The Mists | Proprietor of Parry's Potables. Identified Jain's red vials as healing potions and sold Ravenlost additional potions. |
| **Lavar** | Human | Locust Branch Hospital | Guard at the hospital. Warned Ravenlost that the hospital was dangerous and told them about the disappearances in Crawford. |
| **Pablo** | Dog | Locust Branch Hospital | Lavar's dog. Jain spoke with him. He reported ghosts around the hospital and helped trace Daverick's scent toward the dock. |
| **Cynthia** | Ghost | Locust Branch Hospital | Ghost Pablo said appears around 11:00 PM and slams herself into a hospital window. Ravenlost has heard of her but has not directly met her. |
| **Daverick** | Human | Crawford | Hospital night guard who also tended Crawford Manor's grounds. Disappeared, then was found alive in the manor basement with his tongue removed. Ravenlost rescued him. |
| **Thom Youngblood** | Human | Raven's Claw Inn | Crawford's de facto community leader. Explained the pattern of disappearances and later provided a rough map of Crawford Manor. |
| **Ron** | Human | Raven's Claw Inn | Crawford storekeeper and operator of Larder Supply, which supplied Crawford Manor. |
| **Lone Farmer John** | Human | Crawford | One of Crawford's missing residents. His house was closest to Crawford Manor and was unusually clean when Ravenlost investigated it. |
| **Peterbuild family** | Humans | Crawford | Husband, wife, and son who disappeared from their home. |

| **Persephone Reynolds** | Human | Crawford | Disappeared with her husband Stern. |

| **Ella Lemroy** | Halfling | Crawford | With Earnest, agreed to stay at the inn while Ravenlost used the Lemroy house to trap the mysterious visitor. |
| **Sterling Crawford** | Human | Crawford Manor | Governor of Crawford and husband of Ladna. Became abnormally enormous and unstable as Ladna's condition worsened. |
| **Ladna Crawford** | Human | Crawford Manor | Sterling's wife. Gravely ill and pregnant with an unholy creature. |
| **Thema** | Gnome | Crawford Manor | Elderly caretaker of Crawford Manor. Approximately 300 years old, with stark white hair. |
| **Jennifer** | Human / monster hunter | Cave between Crawford and Waterford | Monster hunter from Mordenshire and Lori's sister. Daughter of Alice, mayor of Mordenshire. |

| **Joan** | Dog | Cave between Crawford and Waterford | One of Jennifer and Lori's hunting dogs. Has silver caps fitted over the teeth. |
| **Tiron** | Dog | Cave between Crawford and Waterford | One of Jennifer and Lori's hunting dogs. Has silver caps fitted over the teeth. |

| **Rudolf von Richten** | Human / monster hunter and herbalist | Van Richten's Herbalist, Mordenshire | Monster hunter and occult expert in Mordenshire. Helped Ravenlost contain a creature released from a mummified cat and shared information about the Mists, Lord Godfroy, and Glim Brightstone. |
| **Farrow** | Human | Waterford | Operates Farrow and Thorn. Hired Ravenlost to recover Lady Waterford's portrait and Lord Waterford's jeweled cane. |

| **Edgar** | Human | Waterford | Gravedigger who regularly checked the Waterford mausoleum. |
| **Renee** | Human | Globe Inn, Waterford | Proprietor of the Globe Inn. Helped Bia and Elisandra learn about Lady Waterford's family history. |

| **Adelaid** | Human | Candle Cross | Elisandra's childhood friend, companion, and "it's complicated." Helped Elisandra escape Candle Cross ten years earlier. |

| **Madeline** | Human, deceased | Blackcard, Mordenshire | Barmaid at the Blackcard and fourth known victim of Mordenshire's murderer. Her body was found near the docks. |
| **Captain Kilm O’Connell** | Human | Mordenshire garrison | Mordenshire captain who recruited Ravenlost to investigate the sanatorium. Received the party’s findings about Peter, the surgeries, Dr. Caroline’s research, and Godfroy’s financing of the restricted wing. |
| **Dr. Caroline Resdonna** | Human, deceased | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Former sanatorium director. Arrived with Dr. Dave about 18 months before Ravenlost infiltrated the facility. | **Dr. Dave** | Human / in custody | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Worked knowingly alongside Dr. Caroline in the ICU and fought Ravenlost during its investigation. Cadric used Suggestion to compel his surrender. Badly burned, survived, and was taken into custody; remained uncooperative. |
| **Dr. Van Larder** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Elderly doctor who has worked at the sanatorium for 43 years. Does not work in the ICU. |
| **Peter** | Human / in custody | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Nightwatchman and Cadric’s undercover supervisor. Evidence linked him to Mordenshire’s murders, including missing daggers. After Bia subdued him at the docks, he remembered only reporting for his shift. Surgical incisions behind his ears and his name in Caroline’s records suggest he was experimented on; whether he acted voluntarily remains uncertain. |
| **Helen** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Nighttime janitor who trained Elisandra on her duties while undercover. |
| **Cookie** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Sanatorium cook. Befriended Cadric and helped him learn Dr. Caroline's eating habits. |
| **Belinda** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Intake nurse who handled Jain's admission and placed her in Wendel's old room. |
| **Randolph** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Lower-security patient from Mordent. Following the sanatorium investigation, Bia checked on him and the other former patients; all three had found work, and she gave each 1 gp. |
| **Janice** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Lower-security patient from Mordent who warned Bia about the ICU. After the investigation, found work; Bia gave her 1 gp. |
| **Robert** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Lower-security patient originally from Barovia, lost in the Mists. After the investigation, found work; Bia gave him 1 gp. |
| **Wendel** | Human | Mordenshire Sanatorium | Scarred, enormously strong patient believed to be the missing ICU patient. Attacked Cadric while Cadric was disguised as Dr. Caroline; killed two orderlies. Became calmer when the disguise ended and was offered food. Survived and was allowed to stay in Caroline’s former quarters and use the garden. His scars suggest experimentation, but successful behavioral conditioning is unconfirmed. |
| **Trashcan** | Human / patient | Mordenshire Sanatorium | High-security patient who likes to sit inside a trashcan. Tried to free and warn other patients during the ICU fire. Later spotted by Jain alone, sitting on a trash barge offshore. |

| **Korath** | Unknown | Bia's past | Name connected to Bia's childhood attack. Identity and significance remain unknown. |

Ravenlost suspects he was her father, but this is unconfirmed. |

| **Osgood Escar** | Human / botanist (apparent) | Welkspring House, Echo Island | Botanist and longtime acquaintance of Glim Brightstone. Bought Welkspring House 73 years ago but appears only in his late fifties. Has an eye patch, damaged left leg, cane, and silver mechanical right hand. | **Chauncey** | Human | Southern shore, Echo Island | Weathered local man who helped moor *The Fletcher* during a storm and gave Ravenlost shelter overnight. Directed them toward Ava for information about the route to Welkspring House. |
| **Ava (former Welkspring groundskeeper)** | Human | Echo Island | Former Welkspring House groundskeeper, released from her position for safety with roughly a year’s wages. | **Welkspring automaton** | Automaton | Welkspring House | Small humanoid-upper-body automaton mounted on a rolling metal ball; strongly resembles Cedgewick. Unable to climb the manor stairs. Builder and connection to Glim remain unknown. |
