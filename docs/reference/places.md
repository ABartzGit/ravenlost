# Places

| **Waterford** | Town | Visited | Larger settlement north of Crawford. Home of the Globe Inn, Farrow and Thorn, and Waterford family mausoleum. |

| **Farrow and Thorn** | Warehouse / business | Visited | Farrow's business in Waterford. Ravenlost received recovery jobs here. |
| **Argent River** | River | Visited | Site of the wrecked merchant vessel containing Lady Waterford's portrait. |
| **Waterford family mausoleum** | Mausoleum / crypt | Visited | Ravenlost met Lord Waterford's ghost and recovered the jeweled cane and Jeremiah's +1 greataxe here. |
| **Waterford Manor** | Former manor | Heard of | Former Waterford family home. No longer exists. |
| **Idlethorp** | Abandoned town | Visited | Former home of the DeLawrence Pouchenelle family. Abandoned when Ravenlost arrived. |
| **DeLawrence Pouchenelle manor** | Manor | Visited / destroyed | Lady Waterford's childhood home. Contained a hidden laboratory and stitched creatures. Jain later burned the manor. |
| **Candle Cross** | Fortified town / hamlet | Visited | Elisandra's hometown, built among ruins of an old castle beside the Arden River. |
| **Candle Cross keep** | Keep | Visited | Seat of Vicar Kellan. Contained Elisandra's preserved room, hidden cavern, and Mist-controlling orb. |
| **Florence's inn** | Inn | Visited | Ravenlost's lodging in Candle Cross. |
| **Arden River** | River | Visited / passed | Candle Cross is built beside it. |
| **Mordenshire** | City / town | Visited / departed | Coastal town and harbor in Mordent. Ravenlost investigated murders and the sanatorium, reported findings to Mayor Alice and Captain O’Connell, received rewards, commissioned equipment, and departed by boat for Echo Island. |
| **Blackcard** | Inn | Visited | Merchant-oriented inn in central Mordenshire. Bia and Cadric stayed here, and Cadric performed in the tavern. |
| **Salty Dog** | Inn | Visited | Inn near the sanatorium. Jain and Elisandra stayed here under the name "Jainisandra." |
| **The Seventh Sea** | Inn / sailors’ tavern | Visited | Coastal tavern in Mordenshire where Ravenlost found Jethro Fletcher unconscious at the bar and met Gobbo, his first mate. Earlier notes call it the Seventh Sea. |
| **Pyrite's Booty** | Shop | Visited | Mordenshire shop where Ravenlost sold much of its accumulated treasure. |
| **Hammerbarn's Blacksmith** | Blacksmith | Visited | Mordenshire smith where Jain left her shortsword to be silvered and Cadric and Elisandra commissioned their EC Lights. |
| **Atim's Arcana** | Magic shop | Visited | Mordenshire magic shop where Cadric purchased an Eversmoking Bottle. |
| **Van Richten's Herbalist** | Herbalist / residence | Visited | Rudolph Van Richten's shop and residence. Contains his research and a basement ritual chamber. |
| **Mordenshire garrison** | Garrison | Visited | Headquarters of Captain Kilm O'Connell. Ravenlost left its weapons and armor here before infiltrating the sanatorium. |
| **Mordenshire Sanatorium** | Sanatorium | Visited / investigation concluded | Three-story facility with basement and restricted ICU. Ravenlost uncovered experimental brain surgeries and records, Dr. Caroline died, Dr. Dave was captured, and evidence linked nightwatchman Peter to the murders. Lord Godfroy financed the restricted addition. |
| **Glaston** | Settlement / region | Heard of | Rumors say druids near Glaston are trying to find a way through the Mists. |
| **Abbey Point** | Coastal location / abbey | Visited / departed | Ravenlost arrived aboard *The Fletcher*, visited an abbey of silent monks devoted to Ezra, and traveled through spider-infested forest to Glim Brightstone's abandoned silver mine. Ravenlost later left the mine and traveled through the Mists toward Falkovnia. |
| **House on Griffin Hill** | Manor / house | Visited | Lord Godfroy's estate outside Mordenshire. Ravenlost noticed apparently spectral groundskeepers, disturbed earth, and strange sounds and vibrations beneath the manor. Godfroy hired Ravenlost here to investigate three leads connected to Glim Brightstone. |
| **Wellspring House** | Manor / botanical estate | Visited / departed | Osgood Escar’s estate on Echo Island, with gardens, a vast greenhouse, animal topiaries, and Gustav the automaton. Ravenlost destroyed a corrupted Golthias blight in the greenhouse and learned about Glim's experiments. At night, blue runes were visible on the gates, fences, and greenhouse framework; whether they prevent ghosts from entering has not been tested. Osgood lent the party a battery fragment and requested news of Glim. |
| **Cauldron of Tepist** | Location | Heard of | Contains fountains associated with resurrection. Godfroy believes they may have inspired part of Glim's apparatus. |
| **Falkovnia** | Domain | Entered / Bia's homeland | Bia's homeland and location of Mikhail Hatzimvas's hospital. Undead hordes began invading approximately six or seven weeks before Ravenlost's arrival. Bia's adoptive father Tobin and sister Mira remain unaccounted for. Ravenlost entered Falkovnia after a long passage through the Mists and headed toward Delmunster. |
| **Griffin Hill** | Region / landmark | Heard of | Associated with Mordent and the Godfroys. |
| **Godfroy's Manor** | Manor | Heard of | Lord and Lady Waterford were traveling toward a gala there when they died. |
| **Old Books** | Bookshop / research | Visited | Mordenshire bookshop where Gilda Haywood researched Osgood Escar and his relationship with Glim Brightstone. |
| **Weathermay Manor** | Manor / residence | Visited | Home of Mayor Alice Weathermay. Located outside of Mordenshire. Ravenlost met with the mayor here to retrieve their reward items. |
| **The Fletcher** | Sailing vessel | Traveled aboard / docked at Abbey Point | Jethro Fletcher's approximately 30-foot, single-masted boat, crewed with Gobbo. Carried Ravenlost from Mordenshire to Echo Island and then Abbey Point. The promised barrel of liquor was unavailable; Bia gave Gobbo 15 gp to deliver to Fletcher, with delivery unconfirmed. |
| **Shauncey’s home** | House / shelter | Visited | Near the southern dock on Echo Island. Ravenlost sheltered here after the storm. Later returned to the sailors' lodging, found Shauncey hung over, and collected cured fish before sailing north. |
| **Ava’s cabin** | Cabin | Visited / revisited | Log cabin on Echo Island where Ravenlost met Welkspring's former groundskeeper. On their return, the party told Ava the blight was destroyed and she could return to the estate, but she preferred to enjoy her year of paid time off. |
| **Welkspring forest shortcut** | Forest path | Traveled | Dangerous route to Welkspring House. Ravenlost fought thorn-shooting and vine-like humanoids before reaching the wrought-iron estate fence. |
| **Welkspring greenhouse** | Greenhouse / botanical collection | Visited | Vast steel-and-glass greenhouse holding rare plants from across Ravenloft, including a locked Death Garden. Osgood's grafted Golthias blight attacked Ravenlost here; after its defeat, the party found black and purple particles entering the water from its roots. Water drained toward the surrounding woods. |
| **Death Garden** | Locked poisonous-plant collection | Visited | Restricted area within Welkspring's greenhouse. Jain picked its lock and narrowly avoided a needle trap, then rested on a chaise lounge after being injured in the blight fight. |
| **Hemlock grove** | Marshy grove | Visited | Downhill from Welkspring House along the greenhouse runoff, toward the coast. White and unusual purple hemlock grew here. Ravenlost fought four dangerous frogs, including an enormous flower-covered king frog, and collected hemlock afterward. |
| **Wooded peninsula north of Abbey Point** | Peninsula / silver mine site | Visited | Not shown on ordinary maps. Osgood visited it with Glim and described a cave with substantial silver deposits, where Glim planned to establish a vault using his "lunar rites." Ravenlost found Glim's mine and hidden vault there. |
| **Abbey Point abbey** | Abbey / religious community | Visited | Home to 17 monks devoted to Ezra who observe vows of silence and communicate in sign language. Davos welcomed Ravenlost; Abbot Katun shared information about Glim, the silver mine, and five missing travelers. The abbey had no alcohol, thwarting the promise made to Captain Fletcher. |
| **Abbey Point forest** | Spider-infested forest | Traveled / fought | Web-covered route from the abbey toward the silver mine. Ravenlost freed an elk, battled a giant spider, rescued Gobbo from a hollow log, and found silk-wrapped victims including Ala'kir, venomous spiderlings, and an undead ogre. |
| **Glim Brightstone's silver mine** | Abandoned mine / silver deposits | Visited / departed | Mine near Abbey Point, formerly operated by Glim and foreman Polk. Contains abandoned rails, a trapped minecart, a broken elevator, Klem's ghost's former resting place, undead miners, and a hidden vault. Ravenlost retrieved Klem's remains from the spider chamber, brought them outside, and departed. |
| **Glim Brightstone's mine vault** | Hidden vault / workshop | Visited / departed | Lower level of the silver mine. Contains damaged automatons, treasure, and a floating dais whose device was missing. An automaton accepted "Osgood" as a password and played Glim's final recording, pointing toward Mikhail Hatzimvas and Kartikoz. Ravenlost rested there before leaving the mine. |
| **Fort Watten** | Falkovnian fort | Heard of from Ala'kir | Fort on Falkovnia's southern border reportedly breached during the undead invasion. |
| **Lekar** | Falkovnian stronghold / settlement | Heard of from Ala'kir | Refugees were reportedly being directed toward this walled city, defended by Commander Korath and the Talons. Matteo described the Red Gate, harsh military discipline, and conscription. Zlava estimated that Lekar was roughly four or five days from the watchtower. Ravenlost has not visited. |
| **Kartikoz** | Location / old inn | Heard of in Glim's recording | Glim said that if his pursuers caught him, "he will take me to Kartikoz at the old inn." The inn, the pursuer, and Glim's fate remain unidentified. |
| **Mikhail Hatzimvas's hospital** | Hospital / Falkovnia | Heard of | Mikhail treats victims of Falkovnia's undead affliction. Osgood supplied a letter of introduction; Glim intended to deliver his final piece to Mikhail. Ravenlost has not visited. |
| **Silver mine spider chamber** | Cavern / web-filled chamber | Visited / departed | High-ceilinged chamber coated in webs, with an opening near the ceiling. Ravenlost fought giant spiders and spider-like humanoids here and recovered Klem's crushed remains and a silver dowsing rod. |
| **Ruined barn in the Mists** | Abandoned barn | Visited | Deteriorating barn with a dead cow bearing bite marks and small cloven footprints. Ravenlost found eleven-year-old Matteo hiding in its hayloft. Elisandra's Augury had advised against staying overnight. |
| **Ravine in the Mists** | Ravine | Crossed | Approximately 30-foot-wide gap with a massive fused-body undead creature moving below. Bia and Jain jumped across, carrying companions in the Bag of Holding with the aid of magic. |
| **Aerie** | Settlement / southern Falkovnia | Heard of from Matteo | Matteo's home settlement. His family fled an undead attack and became separated in the Mists. Its present condition is unknown. |
| **Falkovnian watchtower** | Watchtower / warning post | Visited / departed | Approximately 60-foot tower near the Stone Spine Mountains, with its former entrance almost completely bricked over. Zlava was imprisoned inside to watch for undead hordes and light a warning brazier. Ravenlost rested nearby and later freed her. |
| **Stone Spine Mountains** | Mountain range / landmark | Seen / warning braziers lit | Recognized by Bia on entering Falkovnia; a watchtower nearby held Zlava. After Ravenlost's battle in Delmunster, distant warning braziers burned along the range as far as the party could see. Their immediate cause was not confirmed. |
| **Forest of Strangers** | Forest / travel route | Traveled | Route between the Falkovnian watchtower and Delmunster. Zlava had reported it relatively untouched when she last passed through; Ravenlost traveled onward and reached Delmunster around 3 a.m. |
| **Delmunster** | Settlement / Falkovnia | Visited / current location | Village reached after roughly six hours' travel. Residents walked barefoot; food was abundant despite shortages of other goods. Two adjacent churches, green-tinted oats, mushrooms, and strange synchronized behavior led Ravenlost to a collective organism beneath the condemned church. Ravenlost destroyed it; Annie and Matteo were the only known survivors. The village was deserted after the party's long rest. |
| **Creek Bed Inn** | Inn / tavern | Visited / lodged | Annie's inn in Delmunster, where Ravenlost rented three rooms and ate green-tinged oats. Cadric performed for oddly swaying patrons. After a nap, the party found the inn empty and its kitchen unnaturally clean. |
| **Delmunster churches** | Religious buildings | Visited / investigated | Two adjacent churches: one newer and open, the other condemned. Thurston and Loom taught about the crop and mushrooms. Annie said construction of the newer church began about nine months before Ravenlost arrived. The condemned church concealed stairs leading to the organism's cavern. |
| **Cavern beneath Delmunster's condemned church** | Underground chamber / organism's lair | Visited / battle concluded | Reached by spiral stairs beneath the condemned church and another tunnel near the newer church. Contained a huge fleshy tree-like organism, glowing pods, and assimilated villagers. Jain was restrained here. Ravenlost destroyed the creature; most pods held ooze, while Annie and Matteo survived. A dark orb was left behind. |
| **Delmunster boathouse** | Boathouse / creekside building | Investigated | Held three boats and unusually large quantities of hanging algae. Later the algae disappeared; Cadric broke its lock and found approximately six sets of barefoot human footprints. |
| **Delmunster shop** | General store | Visited | Kenwick supplied Matteo's backpack, shoes, clothes, and wooden cudgel. Food was plentiful, but metal and other supplies were scarce. |
| **Delmunster well and bridge** | Village landmarks | Investigated | Jain examined the well after taking a mushroom and found nothing unusual. Later she sat on the bridge after taking a second mushroom, lost consciousness, and was captured. Villagers subsequently marched in unison toward the well. |
