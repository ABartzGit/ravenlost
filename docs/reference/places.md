| **Idlethorp** | Abandoned town | Visited | Former home of the DeLawrence Pouchenelle family. Abandoned when Ravenlost arrived. |
| **DeLawrence Pouchenelle manor** | Manor | Visited / destroyed | Lady Waterford's childhood home. Contained a hidden laboratory and stitched creatures. Jain later burned the manor. |
| **Candle Cross** | Fortified town / hamlet | Visited | Elisandra's hometown, built among ruins of an old castle beside the Arden River. |
| **Candle Cross keep** | Keep | Visited | Seat of Vicar Kellan. Contained Elisandra's preserved room, hidden cavern, and Mist-controlling orb. |
| **Florence's inn** | Inn | Visited | Ravenlost's lodging in Candle Cross. |
| **Arden River** | River | Visited / passed | Candle Cross is built beside it. |
| **Mordenshire** | City / town | Visited / departed | Coastal town and harbor in Mordent. Ravenlost investigated murders and the sanatorium, reported findings to Mayor Alice and Captain O’Connell, received rewards, commissioned equipment, and departed by boat for Echo Island. |
| **Blackcard** | Inn | Visited | Merchant-oriented inn in central Mordenshire. Bia and Cadric stayed here, and Cadric performed in the tavern. |
| **Salty Dog** | Inn | Visited | Inn near the sanatorium. Jain and Elisandra stayed here under the name "Jainisandra." |
| **The Seventh Sea** | Inn / sailors’ tavern | Visited | Coastal tavern in Mordenshire where Ravenlost found Jethro Fletcher unconscious at the bar and met Gobbo, his first mate. Earlier notes call it the Seventh Sea. |
| **Pyrite's Booty** | Shop | Visited | Mordenshire shop where Ravenlost sold much of its accumulated treasure. |
| **Hammerbarn's Blacksmith** | Blacksmith | Visited | Mordenshire smith where Jain left her shortsword to be silvered and Cadric and Elisandra commissioned their EC Lights. |
| **Atim's Arcana** | Magic shop | Visited | Mordenshire magic shop where Cadric purchased an Eversmoking Bottle. |
| **Van Richten's Herbalist** | Herbalist / residence | Visited | Rudolph Van Richten's shop and residence. Contains his research and a basement ritual chamber. |
| **Mordenshire garrison** | Garrison | Visited | Headquarters of Captain Kilm O'Connell. Ravenlost left its weapons and armor here before infiltrating the sanatorium. |
| **Mordenshire Sanatorium** | Sanatorium | Visited / investigation concluded | Three-story facility with basement and restricted ICU. Ravenlost uncovered experimental brain surgeries and records, Dr. Caroline died, Dr. Dave was captured, and evidence linked nightwatchman Peter to the murders. Lord Godfroy financed the restricted addition. |
| **Glaston** | Settlement / region | Heard of | Rumors say druids near Glaston are trying to find a way through the Mists. |
| **Abbey Point** | Coastal location / abbey | Arrived / current location | Ravenlost reached the dock at approximately 10 PM after a roughly 14-hour voyage aboard *The Fletcher*. Towering walls of Mists flank the approach. A crumbling abbey stands above the shore with candlelight and moving shadows inside; its inhabitants have not yet been encountered. Osgood described a wooded peninsula to the north containing a cave where Glim planned a silver vault. |
| **House on Griffin Hill** | Manor / house | Visited | Lord Godfroy's estate outside Mordenshire. Ravenlost noticed apparently spectral groundskeepers, disturbed earth, and strange sounds and vibrations beneath the manor. Godfroy hired Ravenlost here to investigate three leads connected to Glim Brightstone. |
| **Welkspring**** House** | Manor / botanical estate | Visited / departed | Osgood Escar’s estate on Echo Island, with gardens, a vast greenhouse, animal topiaries, and Gustav the automaton. Ravenlost destroyed a corrupted Golthias blight in the greenhouse and learned about Glim's experiments. At night, blue runes were visible on the gates, fences, and greenhouse framework; whether they prevent ghosts from entering has not been tested. Osgood lent the party a battery fragment and requested news of Glim. |
| **Cauldron of Tepist** | Location | Heard of | Contains fountains associated with resurrection. Godfroy believes they may have inspired part of Glim's apparatus. |
| **Falkovnia** | Domain | Heard of | Location of Mikhail Hatzimvas and his hospital. One of Godfroy's three leads concerning Glim. |
| **Griffin Hill** | Region / landmark | Heard of | Associated with Mordent and the Godfroys. |
| **Godfroy's Manor** | Manor | Heard of | Lord and Lady Waterford were traveling toward a gala there when they died. |
| **Old Books** | Bookshop / research | Visited | Mordenshire bookshop where Gilda Haywood researched Osgood Escar and his relationship with Glim Brightstone. |
| **Weathermay Manor** | Manor / residence | Visited | Home of Mayor Alice Weathermay. Located outside of Mordenshire. Ravenlost met with the mayor here to retrieve their reward items. |
| **The Fletcher** | Sailing vessel | Traveled aboard / arrived at Abbey Point | Jethro Fletcher's approximately 30-foot, single-masted boat, crewed with Gobbo. Carried Ravenlost from Mordenshire to Echo Island and later on a roughly 14-hour voyage to Abbey Point. Jethro expects a promised barrel of liquor and said Abbey Point would be his final destination. |
| **Shauncey’s home** | House / shelter | Visited | Near the southern dock on Echo Island. Ravenlost sheltered here after the storm. Later returned to the sailors' lodging, found Shauncey hung over, and collected cured fish before sailing north. |
| **Ava’s cabin** | Cabin | Visited / revisited | Log cabin on Echo Island where Ravenlost met Welkspring's former groundskeeper. On their return, the party told Ava the blight was destroyed and she could return to the estate, but she preferred to enjoy her year of paid time off. |
| **Welkspring forest shortcut** | Forest path | Traveled | Dangerous route to Welkspring House. Ravenlost fought thorn-shooting and vine-like humanoids before reaching the wrought-iron estate fence. |
| **Welkspring greenhouse** | Greenhouse / botanical collection | Visited | Vast steel-and-glass greenhouse holding rare plants from across Ravenloft, including a locked Death Garden. Osgood's grafted Golthias blight attacked Ravenlost here; after its defeat, the party found black and purple particles entering the water from its roots. Water drained toward the surrounding woods. |
| **Death Garden** | Locked poisonous-plant collection | Visited | Restricted area within Welkspring's greenhouse. Jain picked its lock and narrowly avoided a needle trap, then rested on a chaise lounge after being injured in the blight fight. |
| **Hemlock grove** | Marshy grove | Visited | Downhill from Welkspring House along the greenhouse runoff, toward the coast. White and unusual purple hemlock grew here. Ravenlost fought four dangerous frogs, including an enormous flower-covered king frog, and collected hemlock afterward. |
| **Wooded peninsula north of Abbey Point** | Peninsula / possible hidden vault | Heard of / marked on map | Not shown on ordinary maps. Osgood visited it with Glim and described a cave with substantial silver deposits, where Glim planned to establish a vault using his "lunar rites." Whether the vault exists or contains anything remains unknown. |
| **Abbey Point abbey** | Ruined abbey | Seen from shore / not yet entered | Crumbling abbey above the Abbey Point dock. Ravenlost saw candlelight and shadows moving within. Osgood recalled unsettling monks at an abbey on the peninsula; their identity and intentions are unconfirmed. |
