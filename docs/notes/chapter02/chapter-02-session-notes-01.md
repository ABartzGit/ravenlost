### Lavar

**Lavar** is one of the hospital guards.

He told us:

- The hospital is dangerous.
- Crawford has experienced several recent disappearances.
- **Daverick**, the night guard Lavar was replacing, was missing.
- Daverick should have walked to work.
- There was a town meeting at the Raven's Claw Inn about the disappearances.
- Crawford is governed by **Sterling Crawford**.
- Sterling has been secluded in Crawford Mano since his pregnant wife became ill.

### Pablo

Lavar's dog.

Jain talked to him.

Pablo says there are ghosts around the hospital.

Specific example:

- Every night around **11:00 PM**, a ghost named **Cynthia** throws/slams herself into a hospital window.

Pablo could not smell Daverick along his normal route to work.

He **could** smell Daverick near the dock.

## Crawford Manor

**Sterling Crawford** owns/runs the manor and governs Crawford.

Known information:

- Sterling's wife became ill during pregnancy.
- Sterling withdrew into the manor to care for her.
- The local priest visited when she was approximately **6½ months pregnant**.
- He provided herbs, but they did not help.
- Sterling then banished him from the manor and decided to care for his wife himself.
- Mrs. Crawford had previously been pregnant, but the child did not survive.
- Sterling receives regular shipments from Larder Supply.
- The most recent shipment disappeared and is now four days overdue.
- Typical shipments include grain, meat, and linens.
- Earlier shipments included baby supplies and furniture.
- Requests for baby items eventually stopped.

### Thema

**Thema** is the caretaker at Crawford Mano.

Known information:

- Gnome.
- Approximately 300 years old.
- Roughly Cadric's size/build.
- Stark white hair.
- Apparently rules the manor with an iron fist.

### Daverick's house

Daverick's house was unlocked.

Inside:

- Humble furnishings.
- Unmade cot.
- Back door open.
- Fine porcelain teapot and cup that seemed far too expensive for Daverick.
- Tea with a strong, astringent smell.
- Small white flowers in the teapot.

Outside:

- Large boot print leading toward/into the river.

The priest identified the flowers as **hemlock**.

### Hemlock

- Paralytic.
- Native only to **Echo Island**.
- Same substance later appears to have been used on Cadric.

The priest also agreed that the porcelain looked like something the **Crawford family** could own.

- Same size boot.
- One set of prints was significantly deeper than the other.

- Two empty cups.

  - Medium brown
  - Long blonde

- **Persephone Reynolds:** long blonde hair

## The hooded figure

At approximately **11:30 PM**, Bia saw a roughly four-foot-tall hooded figure emerge from the woods.

It carried:

- Kettle
- Pouch
- Cup

The figure knocked on the Lemroy door.

Cadric answered.

It offered him a cup and said:

**"Drink."**

Cadric was magically compelled to obey.

The tea:

- Tasted vile.
- Paralyzed him.
- Appeared to contain hemlock.

The figure entered and whistled.

### Attack

Bia swung at the figure.

Her axe passed through the cloak.

**There was nothing inside it.**

The figure disappeared, leaving behind:

- Kettle containing the remaining tea
- Lantern

Elisandra heard:

- Something nearby.
- Water moving toward the creek/manor.

Jain saw:

- A **black figure flying toward Crawford Mano**.

### Important

The attacker apparently:

1. Approaches a home.
1. Offers poisoned tea.
1. Compels the victim to drink.
1. Paralyzes the victim.
1. Summons or signals something.
1. Somehow transports the victim toward the river/manor.

This may explain the missing families.
