# Bia's notes - The Child of Iron



---

## Adventure at a Glance

The party left Waterford and continued north toward Mordenshire, stopping in Candle Cross as a storm approached. What should have been an overnight stop became much more complicated when the party discovered that the people of Candle Cross worshipped Elisandra as the **Child of Iron**.

By morning, the town was completely surrounded by the Mists. The party eventually discovered a strange device on the roof of the keep that could pull the Mists into a glass orb. Beneath the keep, they also found a hidden cavern containing six crowned bodies and an altar.

After Jain destroyed the orb, the Mists disappeared and the people of Candle Cross were forced to reconsider everything they had been taught about the Child of Iron. Ravenlost left the following morning, heading north toward Mordenshire with a new invitation from Henry Loust to meet **Lord Godfrey at the House on Griffin Hill**.

---

## Leaving Waterford

- Farrow received the portrait from Cadric.
- The party **did not give Farrow Lord Waterford's jeweled cane**.
- Farrow had already paid each party member **90 gp** for completing one of his two tasks.
- Ravenlost continued north toward **Mordenshire**.
- Beyond Mordenshire is **Abbey Point**, where Glim reportedly went with the mysterious draft.

### Cooper

On the road, we encountered **Cooper**, whose wagon needed repairs.

- Cooper was transporting empty barrels south toward Waterford.
- Bia helped make a temporary repair to his wagon.
- Cadric gave Cooper **2 cp** to deliver to Farrow.
- The money was apparently compensation "for our troubles."
- A dark storm was approaching from the north, so everyone quickly continued on their way.

---

## Candle Cross

We reached **Candle Cross**, a fortified town surrounded by farmland, a river, a moat, high walls, and a drawbridge. A large keep overlooked the town.

Before entering, Elisandra used her necklace to disguise herself. She changed her white hair to black and altered her nose.

That turned out to be a good idea.

### The Child of Iron

In the center of Candle Cross stood an enormous statue, approximately **30 feet tall**, depicting a young woman who looked remarkably like Elisandra.

The statue:

- wore an **iron crown**;
- stood above a platform containing an **iron throne**;
- was acknowledged or prayed to by people passing through the square.

Elisandra finally explained some of her history.

- Elisandra grew up in Candle Cross.
- The people called her the **Child of Iron**.
- They believed she protected the town.
- She had no known father, which contributed to the religious beliefs surrounding her.
- She fled Candle Cross **10 years ago, when she was 18**.
- The town continued worshipping her after she disappeared.
- Her childhood friend and companion **Adelaid** helped her understand that the way Candle Cross treated her was not normal.
- Adelaid helped Elisandra escape.

---

## Florence's Inn

We stayed at an inn run by **Florence**.

Elisandra remained hidden in the room.

**Bia spent:**

- **1 cp** for the room;
- **4 cp** for meals and drinks for Bia and Elisandra.

**Total spent by Bia: 5 cp.**

Cadric spent about an hour performing **"Butterfly"** and other songs on his lute. Afterward, he talked with Florence.

By morning, the drawbridge was up and Candle Cross was completely surrounded by the Mists.

---

## The Mists

The Mists had formed immediately outside Candle Cross, surrounding the town and preventing anyone from leaving.

At the morning sermon, the townspeople were reassured that the **Child of Iron would protect them**.

We decided to investigate.

### Pupert and Mr. Meeples

Jain and Cadric went to the wall.

They met a guard named **Pubert**.

Naturally, they introduced themselves using completely legitimate names:

- Jain was **Pupert**.
- Cadric was **Mr. Meeples**.

Jain attached Cadric to herself in a sort of **baby-Bjorn Cadric**, tied themselves to a rope, and went over the wall.

Pubert begged them not to do it.

When they explained that they were just going to investigate, he looked the other way.

### "I found you"

Inside the Mists, Jain heard someone calling to her.

She saw an injured female Lupine who told her:

**"I found you."**

Jain followed her deeper into the Mists.

Cadric did not see the same thing Jain saw. Instead, he saw what appeared to be another version of Jain.

Cadric convinced Jain to turn back, and they made it safely over the wall.

Jain wanted to try again.

This time, I went with her while Cadric stayed with Elisandra.

I remained on the wall and slowly lowered Jain on the rope until she was just inside the Mists.

Jain saw the female Lupine again. The figure was badly wounded and again said:

**"I found you."**

I felt a hard pull on the rope and immediately hauled Jain back up. Jain hit her head during the trip back and was injured.

Jain tried **one more time**.

I lowered her into the Mists again. The figure appeared directly in front of Jain.

Another pull came through the rope.

This time, I pulled up a nearly lifeless Jain.

I carried Jain to Elisandra, who healed her.

Important

Whatever Jain is seeing in the Mists seems to be specifically connected to her.

We still don't know who the female Lupine is or why she keeps telling Jain, **"I found you."**

---

## Investigating the Keep

We agreed that the **keep** was the most likely place to find answers.

Cadric applied makeup to Elisandra so she could move around outside without immediately being recognized.

Elisandra refused to enter the keep.

- Elisandra remained outside to keep watch.
- Jain stayed with her.
- Bia and Cadric entered the keep and attempted to get a meeting with **Vicar Kellan**.

### Elder Pubert

Inside, Cadric and I met **Elder Pubert**, one of the town's priests.

He is presumably the father of Pubert, the guard on the wall.

Elder Pubert seemed much more helpful than some of Candle Cross's other religious leaders.

### Henry Loust

Cadric and I also unexpectedly encountered **Henry Loust** inside the keep.

Henry had a meeting with Vicar Kellan.

We spoke briefly with Henry before his meeting.

Henry already knew we were traveling with someone who looked remarkably like the Child of Iron. We don't know what Henry told Kellan during their private meeting.

---

## Adelaid

While Jain and Elisandra waited outside, they saw a red-haired woman run past.

Elisandra recognized her as **Adelaid**.

Jain followed her.

Adelaid told Jain that approximately **three weeks earlier**, a large glass sphere had been brought into the keep.

Since then:

- the keep's routine had changed;
- the Vicar and other leaders had become secretive;
- strange fog had begun appearing;
- something clearly wasn't right.

Adelaid could also get into the basement of the keep.

---

## Vicar Kellan

Candric and I eventually met with **Vicar Kellan**.

Kellan claimed that the Mists were a test of faith.

He knew Ravenlost was looking for Henry but did not provide useful answers about what was happening.

Kellan wanted Cadric and me to accompany him.

We refused.

He insisted that we remain in the library.

Then he **locked us in**.

Outside, Jain and Elisandra continued investigating the keep. Jain heard three robed men discussing the need to guard the building.

Ravenlost stopped asking permission.

---

## Breaking into the Keep

Elisandra cast **Silence**.

Cadric picked the lock.

We entered a stairway and encountered a guard.

The guard was knocked unconscious **nonlethally**.

Cadric later searched him and found an **iron crown**.

For once, Ravenlost managed to get through an entire town without murdering anyone.

---

## Beneath the Keep

A fountain in the basement concealed an entrance into a cavern.

Jain entered and discovered **six humanoid figures**.

Something was very wrong with them.

- None appeared to be breathing.
- Their feet were bound.
- Iron crowns had been seared into them.
- Some appeared to have been there much longer than others.

Farther inside was an altar covered in old candle wax.

On the altar sat a **human skull wearing an iron crown**.

Elisandra and Adelaid eventually entered the cavern to find Jain.

Something in the chamber announced that:

**The savior has returned.**

Jain touched the iron crown on the skull.

Nothing happened.

---

## The Second Floor of the Keep

While Jain, Elisandra, and Adelaid investigated below, Cadric and I searched upstairs.

### Elisandra's Room

Elisandra's old room had been preserved for the ten years she had been gone.

It looked more like a museum than a bedroom.

### Vicar Kellan's Room

Cadric picked the lock to Vicar Kellan's room.

The lock was trapped.

Cadric took **1 poison damage**.

Cadric found another **iron crown**.

By the end of the session, Ravenlost had collected **three iron crowns**:

- Cadric collected two.
- Cadric gave one of his crowns to Jain.
- Elisandra took the third crown from the iron throne beneath her statue.

---

## The Orb

I headed directly to the roof.

There I found the glass sphere Adelaid had described.

The device included:

- a large glass orb approximately five feet across;
- rotating metal bands;
- a lever;
- red, yellow, and green buttons;
- a slot containing a power source or battery.

The technology looked similar to equipment we had previously encountered in **Glim's laboratory**.

Cadric joined me on the roof and began experimenting with the controls.

He pulled the lever and pressed the red button.

The Mists began moving toward the keep.

Then the Mists were **sucked into the sphere**.

Something could be seen moving inside the captured Mists.

Whatever was in there was not something Ravenlost wanted getting out.

---

## The Return of the Child of Iron

With the Mists pulled away, the people of Candle Cross began running toward the keep.

Cadric cast **Disguise Self**.

Because Cadric is only three feet tall and the spell can only change his height so much, he couldn't disguise himself as the six-foot-tall adult Elisandra.

Instead, he disguised himself as an approximately **14-year-old Elisandra**, and I lowered him down from the roof on a rope.

I helped convince the crowd that he was Elisandra.

The message was simple:

**The Vicar brought the Mists. The Child of Iron pushed them back.**

The crowd believed it.

Unfortunately, they decided they wanted to kill Vicar Kellan.

We stopped them.

Elder Pubert and others helped restrain Kellan, and approximately ten villagers agreed to guard the keep.

---

## Searching the Keep

I returned the young Elisandra’s clothes to her room.

I then searched the room and found a **bedazzled Bag of Holding**.

It was labeled:

**Eli**

I kept it overnight and gave it to Elisandra the following morning.

---

## Destroying the Orb

We needed to decide what to do with the sphere.

Elisandra cast **Augury** to determine whether destroying it was a good idea.

The answer was favorable.

The detained Vicar Kellan provided them with the destruction sequence:

1. Activate the device.
1. Lower the lever.
1. Press all three buttons.
1. Remove the battery.
1. Get away very quickly.

Jain volunteered. Everyone left the roof for the safety of the ground.

Jain completed the sequence.

Then she had approximately **four to six seconds** to escape.

Jain jumped off the roof into the moat.

The sphere shattered into black pieces.

Something dark escaped upward.

The Mists disappeared.

I ran to the roof and looked down in the moat for Jain.

Jain was floating motionless in the moat.

I went down and pulled her from the water.

Jain was fine.

She was **playing dead**.

---

## Candle Cross After the Mists

With the Mists gone, Elisandra walked out of town and crossed the bridge.

Approximately **40 people followed her**.

For some of them, this was apparently the first time they had ever willingly left Candle Cross.

They celebrated being free.

Unfortunately, they also continued treating Elisandra as their savior.

Everything Elisandra said seemed to become another divine teaching.

Adelaid found the entire situation hilarious.

Eventually, everyone ended up back at Florence's inn.

Elisandra told her followers to have **14 drinks each**, apparently hoping they would get drunk enough to pass out and stop following her.

---

## Vicar Kellan and the Child of Iron

Vicar Kellan was placed in the stocks.

Elisandra confronted him about how she had been treated and groomed as the Child of Iron.

Kellan confirmed that **he had no magic**.

The town's walls were not magically protecting anyone.

Elder Pubert began encouraging the people of Candle Cross to reconsider their beliefs and understand that they could leave the town safely.

We took a long rest.

---

## The Next Morning

Candle Cross was already changing.

People were discussing:

- leaving town;
- traveling;
- exploring places they had never seen.

Adelaid was packed and apparently ready to do exactly that.

I gave Elisandra the bedazzled **"Eli" Bag of Holding** I had found in Elisandra's old room.

We left Candle Cross and continued north.

---

## Henry Loust and the House on Griffin Hill

Outside Candle Cross, we encountered **Henry Loust** again.

Henry gave **me a letter**.

The letter grants Ravenlost access to the **House on Griffin Hill**, where Henry wants the party to meet his master, **Lord Godfrey**.

The party plans to continue north toward **Mordenshire** and eventually visit the House on Griffin Hill.

Henry also pointed us toward Mordenshire's market square for supplies.

### Cadric's Coin

Cadric showed Henry the strange coin connected to **Jordan's disappearance**.

The coin:

- is tarnished silver;
- was found wrapped in a black feather;
- has an engraved symbol that seems to shift when Cadric isn't directly looking at it.

Henry recommended taking it to **Rudolf von Richten**, who runs an herbalist shop in Mordenshire and knows about monsters and the occult. The transcript also confirms that Rudolf is the monster hunter the party had previously been told about.
