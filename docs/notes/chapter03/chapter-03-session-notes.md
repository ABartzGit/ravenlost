# Bia's notes - Shaking things up in Waterford

## Adventure at a Glance

The party left Crawford behind and headed north toward Waterford,
following up on a supply wagon that had disappeared on the road. What
began as a search for a wrecked wagon led to monster hunters, a
mysterious magical stone, a lucrative contract with Farrow and Thorn, a
suspicious portrait recovered from the Argent River, and finally a
late-night expedition into the Waterford family mausoleum. By the end of
the night, the party had fought undead beneath the cemetery, robbed more
than a few graves, battled animated stone guardians, recovered Lord
Waterford’s jeweled cane, and reached **Level 3**. They also left
Waterford with considerably more questions than they started with.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leaving Crawford

- The party spent the night in Crawford following the events at Crawford
  Manor.
- Before leaving, **Cadric performed “Shake It Off” for Jayn**, inspired
  in part by her tendency to shake herself dry.
- Cadric earned **11 copper** in tips.
- The party headed north toward **Waterford**.
- They had also been told to watch for a **missing supply wagon**
  traveling between Waterford and Crawford.
- The road followed **Starling Brook** north.

### The Missing Wagon

About two hours into the journey, **Jayn spotted where a wagon had gone
off the road**. The wagon had:

- gone down an embankment;
- flipped several times;
- scattered food, grain, fishing equipment, and other supplies;
- blood inside;
- signs that someone had been dragged away.

Jayn also found **two sets of relatively fresh humanoid footprints** and
tracks belonging to **two hunting dogs**. Among the wreckage was an
unusually well-secured crate marked with a **raven insignia**. Inside
were:

- children’s clothing;
- medical supplies;
- sterile needles and other medical equipment;
- a smaller locked container.

Inside the smaller container was a **smooth granite stone about the size
of a cue ball, carved with a humanoid face**. The stone was magical.
**Jayn secretly took it without telling the rest of the party.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Following the Tracks

The party followed the tracks from the wagon into the woods and
eventually reached a cave. Inside they discovered the remains of the
wagon attack:

- a badly mauled decaying horse carcass;
- a human corpse that had been partially eaten;
- **two ghouls and a ghast**, already dead.

They also encountered two women and their dogs.

## Jennifer and Lori

The women introduced themselves as **Jennifer and Lori**, sisters from
Mordenshire who hunt monsters.

- **Jennifer** did most of the talking.
- **Lori** was noticeably quieter.
- Their dogs were named **Joan and Tiron**.
- The dogs had **silver caps on their teeth**.

The sisters had been hired by Farrow of **Farrow and Thorn** to
investigate whatever had been attacking travelers in the area. The
evidence indicated that the ghouls and ghast had attacked the wagon and
its occupants. The sisters intended to continue searching the
surrounding woods to make sure nothing else dangerous remained.

### Useful Information

The conversation produced several potentially important leads. Jennifer
and Lori recommended **Rudolf Fanbreaking**, an herbalist in
**Mordenshire** who supplies equipment useful for hunting monsters. They
also mentioned rumors of a **druidic group near Glaston attempting to
find a way through the Mists**. When the party discussed eventually
traveling toward the area associated with **Wilfred Godfrey**, Lori
offered some very simple advice:

> **“Get stronger first.”**

The sisters’ mother is **Alice**, the mayor of Mordenshire. Her position
is an inherited title belonging to her family.

### Memorable Moment

At some point during all of this, **Jayn decided to chew on the dead
horse**. Because Jayn.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Waterford

The party continued north and eventually reached **Waterford**, a
considerably larger settlement than Crawford centered around several
roads and waterways. One of its prominent establishments was the **Globe
Inn**. More importantly, Waterford was home to **Farrow and Thorn**.

## A New Job

The party approached Farrow looking for work. He offered two recovery
jobs.

### Task One — The Portrait

A vessel had wrecked on a sandbar in the **Argent River** north of
Waterford. Somewhere aboard was a particular **portrait** Farrow’s
client wanted recovered.

### Task Two — Lord Waterford’s Cane

The second item was the jeweled walking cane belonging to **Lord
Waterford**. The cane was believed to be somewhere inside the
**Waterford family mausoleum**. After Cadric’s negotiation, the party
would receive: **180 gp each for each completed task.** Farrow also
hinted at a **third task**. He refused to tell the party what it was.
They would only learn about it if they successfully completed the first
two.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Argent River

The party traveled north along the Argent River until they located the
wrecked vessel stranded amid dangerous currents. Getting aboard required
some improvisation involving ropes and **Cadric’s leather straps**. Once
aboard, the party searched the ship.

### Treasure

**Jayn found:**

- a gold necklace with an emerald — **worth 100 gp**;
- **2 copper**;
- a pristine **scimitar**.

**Cadric found:**

- a **silver ring**;
- **200 gp** with an invoice addressed to **Old Books**.

And then they found what they had actually come for.

## The Portrait

The portrait had been carefully wrapped and protected from the water. It
depicted an unidentified woman. Something about it was wrong. Jayne saw
that **the woman’s mouth appeared to move.** The party also began
questioning whether giving the portrait to Farrow was actually a good
idea, and maybe they could get more money returning it to the actual
owner. They brought it back to Waterford anyway—but rather than
immediately turning it over, they smuggled it into their room on the top
floor of the Globe Inn. **Cadric performed downstairs as a distraction
while the portrait was carried upstairs.** For now, the portrait
remained with the party.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Edgar and the Cemetery

The second contract required getting inside the Waterford family
mausoleum. That meant dealing with **Edgar**, who worked at the
cemetery. Cadric approached Edgar under the pretense of being interested
in purchasing a grave plot for his mother. During the conversation, the
party learned about **Idlethorpe**, an abandoned settlement southwest of
Waterford. **Jeremiah Waterford** had apparently been there and
encountered something horrifying involving **many hands**. The party
also learned enough about the cemetery to formulate a plan. They needed
Edgar’s keys. Naturally, they decided to drug him.

## A Night at the Bar

Cadric brought into the Inn Edgar for a drink. **Jayn performed “Jar of
Hearts.”** Meanwhile, flowers previously taken from **Crawford Manor**
were slipped into Edgar’s drink. Eventually, Edgar passed out. The party
acquired his keys.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Waterford Mausoleum

At approximately **11:30 p.m.**, the party entered the Waterford family
mausoleum. Inside they encountered a ghost:

## Lord Waterford

The spirit was **Lord Waterford**. Lord Waterford was aware of
disturbances beneath his family’s graves and ultimately helped guide the
party through the mausoleum. He also gave them directions to the former
**Waterford family home**. There was just one problem.

### Waterford Manor no longer exists.

Lord Waterford seemed completely unaware of that fact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Beneath the Graves

The party descended beneath the mausoleum into a network of cramped
tunnels and burial chambers. Something had been disturbing the graves.
They eventually heard scraping behind a set of doors and prepared an
ambush. For once, the plan was almost organized. Two gaunt, fast-moving
undead creatures emerged. Combat followed. During the exploration and
fighting, **Jayn fell roughly ten feet into the tunnels below** and was
attacked. She was eventually reduced to **0 HP** and had to be rescued
and stabilized. The party survived the encounter and continued deeper
into the Waterford crypts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Grave Robbing with Lord Waterford

Lord Waterford proceeded to identify members of his family buried
throughout the crypt. The party made the decision to rob them. This
created some disagreement about the wisdom—and ethics—of stealing
valuables from the Waterford family while **the ghost of a Waterford was
literally accompanying them**. Nevertheless, the looting began, but only
after Lord Waterford retreated to his tomb.. Among the discoveries:

- Lord Waterford’s cane
- approximately **200 gp** from one tomb;
- a **gold ring**;
- jewelry and necklaces;
- glasses;
- a journal;
- various other valuables.

And then Jayne “accidentally” opened Jeremiah’s tomb.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Stone Guardians

The grave robbing eventually led to another problem. **Large stone
statues came to life.** The party was forced into another close-quarters
battle beneath the mausoleum. The fight included:

- **Bia raging** and engaging the statues in melee;
- **Jayn using Hunter’s Mark**;
- **Cadric using Vicious Mockery**;
- the party using a **Dagger of Disintegration**, which proved
  particularly useful against stone.

The statues were eventually destroyed, crumbling into rubble and dust.
The party survived—barely.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Lord Waterford’s Cane

With the guardians defeated, the party was able to continue searching
the crypts. Eventually they found the second item Farrow had hired them
to retrieve: **Lord Waterford’s jeweled cane.** The cane had:

- a large ruby;
- an obsidian tip;
- gold plating.

The party had now successfully recovered **both items Farrow
requested**. Whether Farrow would actually receive both of them was
another question. And then they searched Jeremiah’s crypt. Inside was a
large magical weapon. Bia’s New Weapon Bia took Jeremiah Waterford’s
magical +1 great axe from Jeremiah Waterford’s tomb.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The Portrait Mystery

Several discoveries during the night made the portrait increasingly
suspicious. The woman in the portrait remains unidentified. However, the
party knows that **Lady Waterford’s grave is empty**. This has led to
the theory that the woman depicted in the portrait might be Lady
Waterford. That has **not been confirmed**. There is another
complication.

### Jayn knows something the others don’t.

Jayn knows the portrait is supposed to be taken:

> **“Home.”**

But Lord Waterford believes the Waterford family home still exists. It
doesn’t. That leaves Jayn—and eventually perhaps the rest of the
party—with no clear idea where the portrait actually wants to go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# End of the Night

After surviving the mausoleum, the party returned to the Globe Inn,
cleaned themselves up, and took a long rest. Most importantly:

## Everyone reached Level 3.

New subclasses and abilities await the party before their next
adventure.

# Unresolved Mysteries

### The Woman in the Portrait

Who is she, and why did her mouth move?

### Lady Waterford

Why is **Lady Waterford’s tomb empty**? Is the woman in the portrait
Lady Waterford?

### “Take Me Home”

Only Jayn knows the portrait is supposed to go “home.” What does that
mean when **Waterford Manor no longer exists**?

### The Missing Manor

Why does Lord Waterford apparently have no idea that his family’s home
is gone? He’d been dead for maybe 60 years? What happened to Waterford
Manor and how long ago?

### Lady Waterford’s Body

If the portrait depicts Lady Waterford, does the party need to find her
remains? Does “home” mean returning her—or the portrait—to the Waterford
family tomb?

### Farrow’s Third Task

What is the task Farrow refused to reveal? Could it have something to do
with Lady Waterford, the portrait, or her missing body?

### The Magical Face Stone

What exactly did Jayn take from the raven-marked medical shipment—and
why was someone transporting it toward Crawford?

### Idlethorpe

What happened to the abandoned settlement, and **what did Jeremiah
encounter there involving all those hands?**

### The Mists

Who are the druids near Glaston, and are they really trying to find a
way through the Mists?
