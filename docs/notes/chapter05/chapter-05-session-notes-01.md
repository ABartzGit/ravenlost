# Bia's notes - Children of the crop



---

## Adventure at a glance

Ravenlost arrived in Delmunster around 3 a.m. and found a village with plentiful food, scarce supplies, barefoot residents, and two neighboring churches. Investigation of strange meals, mushrooms, algae, and a fallen star led the party beneath the condemned church, where an organism had assimilated nearly the entire town. Ravenlost destroyed the creature and rescued Annie and Matteo. After resting, the party saw warning braziers burning along the Stone Spine Mountains and received a message from Lady Estella Godfroy.

Current location: Delmunster, after a long rest at the Creek Bed Inn.

Traveling companion: Matteo, rescued alive after the organism’s destruction.

---

## Arriving in Delmunster

- The party walked roughly six hours and arrived around 3 a.m.
- Two adjacent church spires stood near the edge of town.
- At the Creek Bed Inn, Matteo enthusiastically rang the bell to wake Annie; Ravenlost rented three rooms.
- Breakfast was grayish-green gruel or oats.

### Annie’s news

- Undead were reportedly traveling toward Lekar; some people had been conscripted.
- Commander Korath reportedly came through the Mists about six months earlier.
- Bia remembered hearing the name Korath during the attack on her childhood home and explained the connection to the party.

Unanswered: Is the commander the same Korath Bia heard during earlier attacks? No connection is confirmed.

---

## Investigating the village

### The churches

- Jain and Elisandra noticed that villagers walked barefoot.
- The churches appeared very similar; one was condemned and closed, the other open.
- Thurston explained the villagers’ devotion to the crop and their mushroom observances.
- Jain confessed to Thurston and asked about mushrooms; he referred her to the prophet.
- Annie later said construction of the newer church began about nine months earlier.

Notable remark: Jain observed that she could burn a condemned church and 'this time would be fine.'

### Cadric’s performances

- Cadric performed at the inn with an illusion of a woman washing clothes by a creek.
- Patrons scarcely looked at him and swayed out of time. One said, 'You play music good. Very good.'
- At a later performance, the patrons offered fresh fruit and vegetables instead of coins.

### Bia, Matteo, and the shop

- Matteo asked for a backpack and something sharp to defend himself.
- With no sharp metal weapons available, the shop provided a wooden cudgel, used clothing, shoes, and a backpack.
- The shopkeeper reported plentiful food but shortages of other goods.

### Sir Oinkington

- Bia used Speak with Animals to question livestock. The cow was uninformative.
- Sir Oinkington, a well-spoken pig and local natural historian, said a bright star fell about six months ago.
- According to Sir Oinkington, Teton recovered something from the fall and took it to the prophet.
- Sir Oinkington asked Bia to stop the villagers’ weekly slaughter of a pig; she agreed to relay his request.

Reported, not independently verified: The pig’s account of the starfall and Teton.

---

## The boathouse, Loom, and the mushrooms

- The party regrouped and realized Matteo had slipped away unnoticed.
- Bia checked the boathouse and found three boats and large quantities of algae hanging inside. Annie said creek traffic had dwindled.
- Jain and Elisandra met Loom, the prophet, who was reprimanding Matteo for trying to damage the condemned church with his cudgel.
- Loom asked whether Jain and Elisandra were responsible for Matteo; Jain’s answers were characteristically evasive.
- The pair claimed to be newlyweds on their honeymoon and obtained eight dried mushrooms. Loom recommended starting with one.
- Cadric vaguely remembered Gilda Haywood discussing a 'falling star' and a 'seed.'

Decision: Investigate the condemned church after dark.

---

## The green food and Matteo’s errand

- Bia’s second serving of oats lacked the earlier greenish tint.
- Elisandra gave Matteo five copper to purchase a two-copper sample bowl of food; he could keep the rest.
- Matteo returned about twenty minutes later and said he had spoken with Annie and spent another two copper on porridge for himself.
- The returned sample looked normal, but used bowls in the dining room retained green residue.
- Jain took one mushroom, checked the well, and reported normal water.
- Annie showed the party freshly prepared food without green residue and described the ingredients as oats, well water, salt, and sorghum.

Learned later: Matteo was abducted and assimilated during his twenty-minute errand. The party did not know this at the time.

---

## Jain disappears; the village marches

- Jain took a second mushroom and sat on the bridge; she lost consciousness after feeling the ground fade beneath her. She last saw Matteo watching from the inn window.
- While Jain was outside, Bia and Cadric napped in Bia’s room, Elisandra in hers, and Matteo was assigned Cadric’s room.
- After the nap, the inn was empty and its kitchen abnormally clean. No magic was detected.
- At the boathouse, the hanging algae had vanished. Cadric broke the lock and found roughly six sets of barefoot human footprints.
- Colt, Annie, Thurston, and a fourth villager walked in perfect unison toward the well.
- Four additional groups of four headed toward the condemned church. Matteo was among them.

### Bia infiltrates the procession

- Bia removed her shoes and perfectly copied the villagers’ synchronized movements.
- She followed the procession into the condemned church and down a spiral staircase.
- Elisandra attempted to contact Jain telepathically but received no answer.
- Cadric entered the condemned church stealthily; Elisandra investigated a cellar entrance at the newer church.

---

## The cavern and the collective

- Jain awoke restrained, with a pinkish-green membrane consuming part of one arm.
- The cavern contained illuminated pods and a huge fleshy, tree-like organism.
- Loom described assimilation as consumption of the original body and creation of a duplicate connected to a shared consciousness.
- Loom said the organism fell from the sky and Teton was assimilated first. The creature sought to spread and dominate the realm.
- Jain noticed Bia among the marching villagers and spoke up, accidentally revealing her disguise.
- Loom ordered villagers to seize Bia and search for Cadric and Elisandra.

Important distinction: The party heard Loom’s account of the organism’s origins; the precise timeline of the church’s construction and the starfall remains unresolved.

---

## Battle beneath the condemned church

- Cadric cast Cloud of Daggers against the organism and an attacker; the collective screamed when the central mass was injured.
- Cadric also used a flesh-corrosive poisoned arrow against the organism.
- In the connecting tunnel, Elisandra struck a duplicate Matteo with Guiding Bolt; the injury revealed white ooze instead of flesh.
- Bia raged, fought in close quarters, and shoved an enemy into Cadric’s Cloud of Daggers.
- The organism attacked with razor-edged tentacles; Cadric was knocked unconscious.
- Jain shot the creature’s glowing core, killing it. Its other creatures collapsed.

### Survivors and aftermath

- The pods opened; most contained only ooze.
- Annie and Matteo emerged alive. They were among the most recently assimilated villagers.
- A barrel of green material was found, but the party did not investigate further.
- The darkened orb was left behind. Ravenlost returned to the inn to rest.

---

## The dowsing rod and the warning braziers

| **Dowsing rod use** | **Charges** |
| --- | --- |
| **Locate an object** | 1 |
| **Locate a creature** | 2 |
| **Find a path** | 3 |

The silver rod holds six charges and regains one charge at dawn. Cadric identified it and returned it to the bag of holding.

- After a long rest, Ravenlost found Delmunster empty.
- Burning braziers stretched eastward along the Stone Spine Mountains as far as the party could see.
- Lady Estella Godfroy sent a message asking how Ravenlost acquired the sending stone and saying she needed assistance.
- Jain did not reply, although she considered answering with 'diarrhea.'

---

## Important to remember

- The cause of the green tint in food, the hanging algae, and the barrel of green material was not established.
- The new church reportedly began construction nine months ago; the star reportedly fell six months ago.
- Sir Oinkington asked Bia to intervene on behalf of the pigs.
- Korath’s possible connection to Bia’s childhood remains unproven.
- The warning braziers and Lady Estella’s request may require immediate attention.
- Matteo survived, but his family remains missing.
