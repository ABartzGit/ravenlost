# Bia's notes - It's been a long week



---

## Adventure at a glance

Ravenlost's investigation of the sanatorium came to a fiery conclusion. Dr. Caroline died, Dr. Dave was captured, and the party discovered evidence of experiments involving brain surgery, memory loss, and susceptibility to commands. They also uncovered a connection between the sanatorium's expansion and Lord Godfroy.

After reporting their findings to Mordenshire's authorities, Ravenlost spent several days recovering, collecting equipment, and researching Godfroy's three leads. Information from Gilda Haywood pointed them toward Osgood Escar, a botanist who had known Glim Brightstone and owned Welkspring House on Echo Island.

The journey involved an unusual boat crew, a storm, an overnight stay with Chauncey, and a shortcut through a forest inhabited by hostile plant creatures.

At Welkspring House, Ravenlost encountered an automaton reminiscent of Cedgewick and finally met Osgood. After an uncomfortable conversation about their relationship with Godfroy, Osgood reluctantly invited them inside.

Ravenlost is now inside Welkspring House, hoping to learn more about Glim while keeping dangerous information out of Godfroy's hands.

---

## The sanatorium — fire, escape, and Dr. Caroline

The session resumed in the ICU immediately after Dr. Dave's Fireball. The sanatorium was on fire, Dr. Caroline was unconscious, and the party was scattered across the upper floor.

### The fight in the ICU

- **Jain **was holding an unconscious Dr. Caroline. She dragged her out of the flames rather than leaving her to burn, although she also bit Caroline's cheek.
- **Bia **pursued Dr. Dave through the ICU.
- **Cadric**, still enlarged and disguised as Dr. Caroline, used his appearance to command the orderlies.
- **Elisandra **cast Bless on Bia, Jain, and Trashcan and helped deal with the fire.
- **Trashcan **attempted to unlock patient rooms and free the people trapped inside.
- Dr. Dave hid behind a reinforced door. Cadric attempted Hold Person, but Dave resisted.

## Wendel and the orderlies

The party encountered an enormous, scarred man who appeared to be **Wendel**, the patient who had disappeared into the ICU.

- Wendel initially attacked Cadric because Cadric looked like Dr. Caroline.
- Cadric ordered two orderlies to restrain Wendel, but Wendel killed them.
- Once Cadric dropped his disguise, Wendel became less hostile.
- Wendel had extensive scars and appeared to have been subjected to Dr. Caroline's experiments.
- The party eventually gained his cooperation through food, including Cookie's cookies.

**Important distinction**: Although Wendel had undergone some form of experimentation, we have no evidence that the procedures successfully made him susceptible to commands, as they had the orderlies.

### Dr. Caroline's death

Jain later discovered that Dr. Caroline had stopped breathing. She checked her pulse and considered whether she was supposed to heal her, but Caroline died before the guards arrived.

Jain dressed Caroline's body so the guards would not find her naked.

This left Ravenlost without the opportunity to question Dr. Caroline directly about her research or her arrangement with Godfroy.

### Dr. Dave's capture

Dr. Dave was eventually captured after Cadric successfully used **Suggestion **to make him surrender.

- Dave was badly burned and required medical attention.
- Unlike the orderlies and other patients, Dave appeared to be a willing aide in Dr. Caroline's work.
- He knew Wendel and had access to the ICU.
- He was uncooperative when questioned after the spell ended.
- The authorities took custody of him.

### The patients

- Ravenlost helped get patients out of danger during the fire.
- Trashcan disappeared during the confusion.
- Wendel survived and was later allowed to stay in Dr. Caroline's former quarters, with access to the garden.
- Dr. Goth, the party's nickname for another doctor, helped care for Wendel.

## What we found in Dr. Caroline's rooms

After the fighting in the ICU, Ravenlost searched Dr. Caroline's quarters for evidence of what had been happening inside the sanatorium.

***Control: A Roadmap***

The most important discovery was a book titled **Control: A Roadmap**, which helped explain the disturbing behavior of the orderlies and the condition of several patients.

The research appeared to involve surgically altering people to make them more susceptible to suggestions or commands.

- The orderlies had similar scars on the tops of their heads, suggesting they had undergone the same procedure.
- The orderlies responded to commands from someone they believed was Dr. Caroline, as Cadric had demonstrated while disguised as her.
- Wendel had extensive surgical scars, but he did not exhibit the same obedient behavior.
- The experiments appeared to involve repeated attempts to achieve a particular result.

**What we know**: The sanatorium was being used for experiments involving behavioral control, and the orderlies appeared to be successfully altered subjects.

**What we don't know**: Exactly how the procedures worked, how many people were experimented on, or whether the effects could be reversed.

### Dr. Edgar Iskander

Jain and Elisandra found several items connecting Dr. Caroline to **Dr. Edgar Iskander**.

- **Doctor's robes**: Found in a locked chest, embroidered with Dr. Edgar Iskander's name and associated with Locust Branch Hospital in Crawford.
- **Pencil sketch**: Depicted a man holding a young child. On the back was written: “To my dear star, I’ll make the world a safer place for you.”
- **Research journal**: Control: A Roadmap, written by Dr. Edgar Iskander approximately 40 years earlier. The handwriting matched the message on the sketch.

The journal indicated that Edgar had lost his funding at Locust Branch Hospital after a surgery went wrong and a patient became violent.

**Our theory**: Edgar was Dr. Caroline's father, and she continued his research. The evidence is compelling, but we haven't confirmed their relationship.

### Dr. Caroline's patient records

The records contained numerous names, many crossed out or marked as failed experiments or unsuitable candidates.

| **Patient** | **What we learned** |
| --- | --- |
| Wendel | Listed among the experimental subjects; had extensive scarring |
| Peter | His name appeared in the records |
| Trashcan | His entry contained several question marks |
| Anne | Recorded as having died during surgery |
| Jain | Described as a “Lupine — promising candidate” |

Dr. Caroline had apparently identified Jain as a potential subject almost immediately after her admission.

### Lord Godfroy's involvement

The party discovered architectural plans for the sanatorium's remodeled wing, including the restricted ICU.

Lord Godfroy had privately funded the addition.

This was the first concrete evidence connecting him to the sanatorium's activities.

Later, Henry Loust confirmed that Dr. Caroline and Godfroy had an arrangement: Godfroy financed the new wing in exchange for Caroline supplying things he needed.

Ravenlost suspects Caroline intended to perfect Edgar's research and provide the results to Godfroy.

However, we still don't know exactly what Godfroy requested, how much he knew about the experiments, or how he intends to use the research.

### Questions to investigate later

- Was Dr. Edgar Iskander actually Dr. Caroline's father?
- What happened to Edgar after he lost his funding at Locust Branch Hospital?
- Can the effects of the experimental surgeries be reversed?
- What exactly did Godfroy expect Dr. Caroline to supply?
- Was Godfroy interested in controlling living people, or does this research serve another purpose?

## Peter and the Mordenshire murders

After uncovering Dr. Caroline's experiments, Ravenlost still needed to solve the murders that had brought them to the sanatorium in the first place.

### The missing nightwatchman

Peter, the sanatorium's nightwatchman and Cadric's supervisor, had disappeared from his post.

Ravenlost investigated his quarters and discovered several suspicious details:

- His bed appeared untouched.
- A chair had been placed in the room.
- A worn circular path suggested someone had been pacing repeatedly.
- A case designed to hold **seven daggers** contained only five.

The missing daggers were particularly concerning given the murders around Mordenshire.

Ravenlost alerted the guards, who helped secure the area while the party followed Peter's trail.

### The confrontation at the docks

Peter's trail eventually led to the docks.

Before pursuing him, Ravenlost retrieved their weapons and equipment from the garrison, where they had left them before entering the sanatorium undercover.

At the docks, Peter attempted to escape.

- Jain tried to shoot him with an arrow, but she missed and hit a drunk soldier instead.
- Bia pursued and subdued Peter.
- During the confrontation, Peter stabbed Bia.
- Bia knocked Peter unconscious against a wooden piling.
- Peter survived and was taken into custody.
- Cadric applied some aid to the drunk sailor - **Captain Jethro Fletcher**.

The evidence strongly suggested that Peter was responsible for the murders, but Ravenlost had begun to suspect he might not have been acting voluntarily.

### Peter's condition

When questioned afterward, Peter appeared confused.

- He remembered reporting for his night shift at the sanatorium.
- He did not remember leaving his post.
- He had no memory of fleeing to the docks, attacking Bia, or being captured.
- The guards discovered **surgical incisions behind his ears**.

These incisions were different from the scars on the orderlies' heads, but they suggested Peter had also undergone some form of experimental surgery.

**Our conclusion**: Peter was almost certainly the murderer, but Dr. Caroline may have manipulated him into committing the crimes.

We don't know whether Peter was aware of his actions at the time or whether his missing memories can be recovered.

## Reporting to the authorities

Ravenlost met with **Mayor Alice Weathermay** and **Captain O'Connell** to report what they had discovered.

### Evidence presented

The party explained or showed the authorities:

- Dr. Edgar Iskander's research journal, Control: A Roadmap.
- The experiments involving memory, aggression, and susceptibility to commands.
- The identical surgical scars on the orderlies.
- Peter's missing memories, surgical incisions, and connection to the murders.
- Dr. Caroline's death and Dr. Dave's capture.
- The architectural plans showing that Lord Godfroy financed the sanatorium's restricted wing.

Captain O'Connell confirmed that the orderlies did not speak and had identical scarring around their skulls.

The authorities also identified a deceased patient in the morgue as **Anne Polk**, who had been at the sanatorium for approximately three years.

### Dr. Dave's involvement

Dr. Dave had arrived at the sanatorium with Dr. Caroline and worked alongside her in the ICU.

Unlike Peter and the orderlies, there was no indication that Dave had been subjected to the experiments against his will.

He appeared to have participated in these experiments on patients knowingly.

Although he survived the confrontation with Ravenlost, he was badly burned and remained uncooperative when questioned.

### What happens next?

With Dr. Caroline dead and Dr. Dave in custody, the sanatorium's leadership had effectively collapsed.

The authorities now had evidence of serious wrongdoing, but several questions remained unanswered:

- How many patients had been experimented on?
- How many had died?
- What would happen to the surviving patients?
- Could Peter be held responsible for murders he might have committed under someone else's control?
- Would Dr. Dave eventually reveal what he knew?
- Would the authorities investigate Godfroy's financial involvement?

Ravenlost had solved the immediate murder mystery, but the investigation had exposed something considerably larger.

Gilda's research also identified hemlock growing along the northwestern coast of Echo Island.

Cadric was particularly interested in obtaining some.

Before departing, Ravenlost dealt with their remaining equipment and commissions.

## The voyage to Echo Island

Ravenlost arranged passage to Echo Island aboard The Fletcher, a small sailing vessel captained by Jethro Fletcher.

### Jethro and Gobbo

The party found *The Fletcher* at the Mordenshire docks, but Jethro wasn't aboard.

They eventually located him at the **Seven Seas**, a tavern popular with sailors.

- Jethro was passed out at the bar, holding a mug.
- **Gobbo**, a small green goblin, was sitting nearby on a stool.
- Gobbo introduced himself as Jethro's first mate.
- When asked to wake the captain, Gobbo kicked Jethro with both feet, knocking him off his stool and onto the floor.

Jethro recognized Cadric, who had previously healed his injured leg.

He was also acquainted with Jain, but he didn’t know it. She had shot him.

### Negotiating passage

Jethro agreed to take Ravenlost to Echo Island.

- **Vessel**: The Fletcher, approximately 30 feet long, with a single mast.
- **Destination**: The southern shore of Echo Island.
- **Estimated travel time**: Approximately 12 hours, weather permitting.
- **Limitation**: Jethro refused to approach Welkspring House itself.
- **Payment**: Jethro initially offered passage for free because Ravenlost had helped him previously.

The party insisted on paying and agreed to provide **six cases of liquor**.

Each case cost 6 sp, for a total of **36 sp**.

The payment arrangement was:

| **When** | **Cases** |
| --- | --- |
| Before departure | 1 |
| Upon arrival at Echo Island | 2 |
| When Jethro returned to collect Ravenlost | 3 |
| Total | 6 |

Jethro and Gobbo would return to pick up Ravenlost when the party was ready to leave.

### The storm

The first half of the voyage was relatively uneventful.

Then the weather deteriorated.

A powerful storm struck The Fletcher, forcing everyone to help keep the small boat under control.

- **Bia **held one of the main lines, although she needed Jethro to point out which rope he meant.
- **Gobbo **ended up hanging from the end of the rope Bia was holding.
- **Jain **and **Elisandra **helped steady the rudder.
- **Cadric **attempted to encourage everyone with a sea shanty, but his performance wasn't particularly effective.
- **Jethro **managed to guide the boat safely to the southern dock.

Despite the storm, everyone survived the crossing.

## Echo Island

### Meeting Chauncey

Ravenlost arrived at a small dock on Echo Island's southern shore.

The area contained a few buildings, fishing boats, and cliffs overlooking the water.

An older man named **Chauncey**, wearing a weathered raincoat and carrying a shielded lantern, came out to help secure *The Fletcher*.

Chauncey offered the party shelter for the night.

When he asked why they had come to Echo Island, Ravenlost avoided mentioning their real assignment.

Jain provided an explanation:

“Grass. I'm a fan of grass, and I've heard there's exquisite grass here.”

The party also claimed to be a traveling circus troupe.

Cadric performed his new song, which Chauncey thoroughly enjoyed.

Ravenlost spent the night at Chauncey's home without incident. They did not establish a watch.

By morning, the storm had passed.

### Getting directions to Welkspring House

Chauncey explained that Welkspring House was approximately two hours inland, beyond a forest.

He warned Ravenlost that strange things had been happening in the woods and suggested speaking with Ava, a former groundskeeper at Welkspring House.

Ava might know more about the estate and the surrounding area.

---

## Ava, the former groundskeeper

Ravenlost found Ava working near a log cabin, chopping wood.

She was a muscular woman with short brown hair who had previously worked at Welkspring House.

### What Ava told us

- Ava had worked as a groundskeeper at Welkspring House.
- She had been released from her position for her own safety and received approximately a year's wages.
- She had not returned to the estate since leaving.
- For roughly six months, she had occasionally glimpsed strange humanoid figures moving through the forest.
- Some figures were small, some tall and thin, and others broader.
- The creatures left no tracks that Ava could find.
- She did not know exactly what they were.

Ava described two possible routes to Welkspring House:

| **Route** | **Description** |
| --- | --- |
| Established path | Longer, but considered safer |
| Forest shortcut | Faster, but more dangerous because of the unidentified creatures |

Ravenlost chose the shortcut.

Naturally.

## The forest shortcut

The forest was unusually quiet.

Jain eventually spotted a humanoid figure peeking from behind a tree.

Cadric attempted to communicate using a birdcall, but something responded by firing a **three-inch thorn into his neck**, causing 9 piercing damage.

### The creatures

Ravenlost encountered several unfamiliar plant-like humanoids.

- Smaller creatures fired sharp thorns or needles.
- Larger creatures appeared to be composed partly of vines.
- One larger creature restrained Bia.
- Jain severed one creature's arm, but it regenerated.
- Several creatures were defeated, while approximately three escaped into the forest.

The party did not pursue the fleeing creatures.

**We don’t  know what these creatures are or whether they have any connection to Osgood or Welkspring House.**

### Reaching Welkspring

Approximately half an hour later, Ravenlost reached the estate's perimeter.

- The property was enclosed by a wrought-iron fence approximately **8 feet tall**.
- Tall, carefully maintained hedges stood beyond the fence.
- Ravenlost followed the perimeter until they found an open gate.

Before entering, Cadric pulled the thorn out of his neck.

He kept it rather than throwing it away because someone had ideas about what to do with it later.

## Welkspring House

After surviving the forest shortcut, Ravenlost entered the grounds of **Welkspring House**, hoping to find Osgood Escar and learn more about his connection to Glim Brightstone.

### The estate

The grounds were surprisingly well maintained, especially considering the creatures lurking in the surrounding forest.

- A winding path led through immaculate gardens toward a large manor.
- An enormous greenhouse stood beside the house, filled with greenery.
- A topiary garden contained carefully trimmed animal figures.
- The manor's front entrance had a raised porch, two staircases, and large French doors.

Ravenlost considered investigating the topiary animals but decided against it.

We had encountered enough dangerous plants for one day.

### A familiar automaton

When Ravenlost knocked on the manor's doors, a small automaton answered.

- Approximately 3½ feet tall.
- Humanoid upper body.
- A large metal ball instead of legs.
- Construction strongly reminiscent of Cedgewick, the automaton Ravenlost discovered in Glim Brightstone's laboratory.

The resemblance was immediately apparent to everyone.

Ravenlost mentioned both Glim and Cedgewick to the automaton, which offered to fetch its master.

Unfortunately, it couldn't climb stairs.

It repeatedly rolled into the bottom step until a voice called from upstairs.

Eventually, Osgood Escar came down to meet the party.

**Important**: We don't know who built this automaton or whether it has any direct connection to Cedgewick.

---

## Osgood Escar

### Appearance

Osgood appeared to be in his late fifties.

- Salt-and-pepper hair.
- An eye patch.
- Gardening clothes that appeared well worn and dirty.
- A badly gnarled left leg requiring a cane.
- A missing right hand, replaced by a bright silver mechanical hand.

The mechanical hand appeared capable of moving independently.

**Something doesn't add up**: Gilda's research established that Osgood purchased Welkspring House 73 years ago, yet he looks considerably younger than that.

Ravenlost noticed the discrepancy but did not question him about it.

### Our conversation with Osgood

Cadric began by telling Osgood that Lord Godfroy had sent Ravenlost.

Osgood immediately became guarded.

The conversation was difficult because Ravenlost couldn't offer a simple explanation of its relationship with Godfroy.

We told Osgood:

- We had discovered one of Glim Brightstone's laboratories.
- Godfroy was interested in Glim's inventions, particularly an augmented version of the batteries used to power his automatons.
- We had encountered a Glim-related device beneath Candle Cross that could manipulate the Mists surrounding the town.
- We suspected Godfroy intended to use Glim's technology to restore himself to life.
- We were working on Godfroy's assignment but did not actually want to help him accomplish that goal.

Osgood was understandably skeptical.

### Glim Brightstone

Ravenlost also discussed Glim, whom Osgood had known many years earlier.

- Osgood acknowledged his friendship with Glim.
- His reaction suggested genuine affection.
- Ravenlost explained that Glim had been missing for many years.
- We shared what we knew about Glim's laboratory and his apparent connection to the devices Godfroy wanted.

We still don't know whether Osgood has any information about Glim's current whereabouts.

### Why Osgood should trust us?

Cadric and the others tried to explain that Ravenlost wasn't necessarily helping Godfroy simply because we had accepted his assignment.

We also pointed out that Godfroy already knew about Welkspring House.

If Ravenlost returned without useful information, he might send someone else—possibly someone less interested in protecting Osgood or Glim's research.

The party also explained something important about Cadric's equipment.

Godfroy's magical shortsword:

- Godfroy had loaned Cadric a magical +1 shortsword.
- Ravenlost discovered that Godfroy could use the weapon to scry on Cadric.
- The party placed it inside the Bag of Holding to prevent that surveillance.
- Cadric offered this as evidence that Ravenlost wasn't simply reporting everything back to Godfroy.

Eventually, Ravenlost made its position as clear as possible.

**We are carrying out Godfroy's assignment, but we do not want to help him return to life and bring death and misery to others.**

We just haven't figured out how to stop him.

### The invitation

After listening to Ravenlost's explanations, Osgood finally agreed to let us enter the manor.

He had one instruction:

“Come inside. But don't touch anything.”

Ravenlost entered Welkspring House.
