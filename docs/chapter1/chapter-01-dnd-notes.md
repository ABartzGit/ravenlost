# Notes

Four strangers answered the promise of two hundred gold. Before the job was finished, they would cross a forest after dark, enter a ruined church transformed into an alchemical laboratory, awaken a sixty-five-year-old automaton, conceal a discovery that could threaten the Domains of Dread, and learn that neither their employer nor the inn in which they met was what it seemed.

## Ravenlost

The four adventurers who would come to be known as **Ravenlost** met for the first time at the Black Lantern Inn.

- **Bia Galene**, a six-foot-six female orc barbarian.
- **Cadric Veyl**, a three-foot-tall halfling bard with sandy hair and hazel eyes, weighing only thirty-two pounds.
- **Jayn Blight**, a six-foot-four female wolf ranger with gunmetal-colored fur, a purple left eye, and a scar crossing her right eye.
- **Elisandra Corvis**, a six-foot-tall Kalashtar cleric with pure white hair.

They arrived as strangers. Each had followed word of a job worth two hundred gold.

By the time they returned, they were not yet friends, but they were beginning to become a company - Ravenlost.

## New faces

### Henry Loust

An elderly-seeming gentleman in fine but worn clothing who hired Ravenlost on behalf of Lady Estella Godfroy. He was later revealed to be incorporeal, though the nature of his connection to the Black Lantern Inn remains unknown.

### Lady Estella Godfroy

Henry's patroness and a member of the Mordent Godfroy family. She wanted Glim Brightstone's journal recovered.

### Glim Brightstone

A dwarf alchemist who disappeared nearly sixty-five years ago. He developed an apparatus that could potentially chart a path through the Mists and break a dreadlord's hold over them.

### Cedgewick

An automaton created by Glim Brightstone. Glim used him to preserve journal entries on wax recording tubes. Much of Cedgewick's memory was deliberately erased.

### Victor Mordenheim

The dreadlord of Lemordia and one of several figures believed to have wanted Glim's apparatus.

### Wilfred Godfroy

The dreadlord of Mordent, toward whose domain Glim charted his final known course.

### Ava

A fortune teller who entered the abandoned Black Lantern Inn immediately after the party discovered its true condition.

## Bestiary and field notes

### Wolves

A pack of four attacked Ravenlost shortly after Jayn answered a distant howl.

### Animated mechanical remains

Separate arms, legs, and torsos that moved independently after the laboratory's systems were activated.

### Metal-eating construct

A small mechanical, animal-like creature attracted to metal and capable of damaging it. Jayn temporarily kept it on a rope.

### Unknown toe-walking creatures

Five creatures crashed through the laboratory's stained-glass roof. Their highly arched feet matched the unusual tracks found in the woods and around the bodies. Cedgewick identified them as the work of Victor Mordenheim.

## Discoveries

- Glim Brightstone stored his journal entries on wax tubes recorded through Cedgewick.
- Lunar energy could be focused to plot a route through the Mists.
- A sufficiently charged apparatus might break a dreadlord's control over the fog.
- The apparatus also affected the physical and spiritual nature of a person.
- Glim feared his invention and eventually fled north toward Mordent.
- Someone deliberately erased portions of Cedgewick's memory.
- Glim had an assistant whose identity is currently unknown.
- Glim believed woods existed beyond Abbey Point, despite the map ending there.
- Henry Loust and the two missing treasure hunters were spirits.
- The Black Lantern Inn may not exist in the ordinary way—or may not have been occupied for many years.

## Bia's journal

We left the inn as strangers.

Cadric talked Henry into paying us half before we went. Jayn drank four bowls of grog before we even reached the road. Elisandra watched everything and carried her own light into the dark. I carried my axe.

The footprints bothered me. Five toes, but no heel. I had never seen tracks like them, and I didn't like knowing that whatever made them was somewhere ahead of us.

I screamed when the arm reached for me. I will deny this if Cadric writes a song about it.

Cedgewick was not what I expected to find in that place. He had been alone for almost sixty-five years, surrounded by Glim's work and missing pieces of his own memory. We asked him to leave with us, but he couldn't. I understand what it means to be bound to a home, though I don't know whether his bond is loyalty or a cage.

We destroyed three recordings. I think it was the right choice. Knowledge that can tear through the Mists and change a person's soul shouldn't be handed to someone simply because he promises gold.

The ghosts agreed with our story. That was fortunate.

Henry was a ghost too. None of us noticed. Jayn noticed the moving pages, but apparently didn't think it worth mentioning until after Henry walked through the table.

We earned our gold. We are stronger now than when we left. I'm not sure whether that means we are ready for what comes next.

When we opened Henry's door, the inn was dead and covered in dust.

Then Ava arrived.

I still don't trust the Mists. I am beginning to think I may trust the others.

## Looking ahead

The Black Lantern Inn was empty. Dust muffled every step, and the laughter that had filled the taproom moments earlier seemed impossible.

A knock sounded at the door.

No one answered.

The door opened anyway.

Ava the fortune teller stepped out of the Mists and into the abandoned inn.
