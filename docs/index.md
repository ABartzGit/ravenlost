# The Adventures of Ravenlost

*Four strangers. One very bad mist problem.*

<img src="images/ravenlost.png" class="chapter-art" alt="Ravenlost group">

Bia, Cadric, Jain, and Elisandra were strangers when they arrived at the Black Raven Inn, each responding to the same job offer.

What started as a simple job has taken them through the Mists, where they've uncovered strange mysteries, battled monsters, encountered more than a few ghosts, and occasionally made extremely questionable decisions.

## Start the adventure

It all began at the Black Raven Inn, with four strangers and a job from a man named Henry Loust.

[**Read Chapter 1: Four strangers in the Mists →**](chapters/chapter01/chapter-01-the-black-lantern-inn.md)

## Where are they now?

<div class="campaign-status">

<p><strong>📍 Near the Stone Spine Mountains in Falkovnia</strong> &nbsp;&nbsp; <strong>⚔️ Level 5</strong></p>

<p>
After escaping a spider-infested mine, Ravenlost ventured through the Mists, 
rescued a young boy named Matteo, and witnessed the horrors of an undead 
invasion. They also freed an elderly prisoner from a watchtower and learned 
that Commander Korath—a name haunting Bia's past—rules Falkovnia with an 
iron fist.
</p>

<p><strong>Next:</strong> With Matteo in tow, Ravenlost heads through the 
Forest of Strangers toward Delmunster. But with undead hordes roaming the 
countryside, Bia's family still missing, and a warning not to trust the 
commander, getting to Lekar may prove more dangerous than getting into 
Falkovnia.</p>

</div>
