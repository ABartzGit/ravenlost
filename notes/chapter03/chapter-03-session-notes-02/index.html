    Bia's notes - Home is where the horrors are

    Adventure at a glance

    • Investigated Lady Waterford’s past and learned that she was born a DeLawrence Pouchenelle and came from Idlethorp.
    • Followed Lady Waterford’s portrait to the abandoned town of Idlethorp and her family’s ruined manor.
    • Fought grotesque creatures made from stitched-together body parts and discovered they were connected to a living brain in a jar.
    • Encountered a huge stitched man who resisted whatever was controlling him long enough to remove the bolts from his neck. His final word was “Sister.”
    • Returned Lady Waterford’s portrait to its place among the DeLawrence Pouchenelle family portraits.
    • Bia destroyed the brain, causing the remaining stitched creatures to collapse.
    • Looted several magic items and potions before Jain burned down the manor.
    • Returned to Farrow and received 90 gp each as partial payment.
    • Learned Farrow’s mysterious third job may have involved retrieving the very brain Bia had just destroyed.
    • Left Waterford and headed toward Candle Cross, where the castle drawbridge reportedly moves on its own.

    Waterford

    • Woke up still trying to figure out what Lady Waterford meant when she told Jain she wanted to go home.
    • Jain stayed in room 301 with the portrait.
    • Cadric questioned Edgar.
    • Elisandra and I talked to Renee, who owns the Globe Inn.
    • Learned:
    • Lord and Lady Waterford died in a carriage accident 64 years ago.
    • They were traveling toward Mordenshire for a gala at Godfroy's Manor.
    • Lady Waterford was a DeLawrence Pouchenelle before marrying Lord Waterford.
    • Her family was from Idlethorp.
    • Edgar checks the Waterford mausoleum regularly. There is a sealed coffin for Lady Waterford, but he has never opened it.
    • We already know her coffin is empty. So where is her body?

    Scarred ghost

    • While Jain was alone in room 301, a scarred man came into the room.
    • He seemed surprised to find Jain there.
    • Then he walked through the wall.
    • Jain saw him heading toward the docks.
    • I stayed with Lady Waterford's portrait while everyone else went after him.
    • They found a secret gathering place under the Globe Inn. We called it the human speakeasy.
    • Didn't figure out who the scarred man was, but the group did recognize one of the ghosts as one who was in Henry Loust’s office.

    Idlethorp

    • Headed to Idlethorp because that might be the "home" Lady Waterford wanted.
    • Red warning flags outside town.
    • Met Dave, who was warning travelers away.
    • Town has been abandoned for about 6 weeks.
    • Strange noises and shadowy creatures at night.
    • Another group went in about a week ago. Only one came back.
    • We camped outside town instead of going in at night.
    • I suggested taking out Lady Waterford's portrait to see if she reacted.
    • Jain touched the frame and suddenly ran toward the manor with the portrait.
    • She wasn't controlling herself and didn't remember running there afterward.

    Creatures in Idlethorp

    • Attacked by creatures made from stitched-together human body parts.
    • Cadric used Daylight.
    • The creatures hated it and fled.
    • They all ran toward the DeLawrence Pouchenelle manor.

    DeLawrence Pouchenelle Manor

    • Jain pressed Lady Waterford's portrait against the manor and asked if this was the home she wanted.
    • Jain felt warmth/contentment. Definitely the right place.

    Giant stitched man

    • Found a stitched humanoid over 8 feet tall.
    • Two metal bolts sticking out of his neck.
    • He attacked us.
    • Elisandra spoke to him telepathically about Lady Waterford and the DeLawrence Pouchenelle family.
    • He seemed to recognize something.
    • Electricity came from the bolts and seemed to take control again.
    • Elisandra told him the bolts might be controlling him.
    • He pulled both bolts out himself.
    • Electricity arced through him.
    • He collapsed and said "Sister."
    • He died.
    • Thick, dark red sludge came out of him instead of normal blood.
    • Who was he? Whose sister was he remembering? Lady Waterford?

    Meanwhile, Jain...

    • Jain ended up alone in the ruined ballroom.
    • Huge pile of wagons, crates, lumber, dead horses, body parts, etc.
    • Watched a torso creature crawl into the pile.
    • Jain actually decided NOT to investigate it.
    • This seems important enough to document.
    • Instead, she investigated the fireplace.
    • Found a pressure-activated hook above the fireplace mantel.
    • Climbed up and triggered it.
    • Opened a secret passage.
    • Went inside by herself, because apparently the good judgment had worn off.

    Secret rooms / laboratory

    • Cadric picked a lock.
    • Swinging blade dropped from the ceiling and barely missed his head.
    • Heard a voice say: "Don't disturb him while he's working."
    • Found a library/laboratory.
    • Man working over the body of a dwarf with surgical tools.
    • Leon DeLawrence Pouchenelle — Lady Waterford's brother.
    • Also: living brain in a jar.
    • Brain attacked me psychically.
    • More stitched creatures surrounded us.

    Ravenlost vs. the brain

    • I eventually smashed the brain's jar with my axe.
    • Brain exploded.
    • All of the remaining stitched creatures collapsed.
    • Apparently the brain was controlling/animating them.
    • At the time, destroying the evil brain seemed like an excellent idea.

    Jain returns

    • Jain came out through a bookcase connected to her secret fireplace passage.
    • Lots of dismembered body parts everywhere after the brain died.
    • Jain picked up two hands/arms.
    • Waved them around at Cadric.
    • A ring flew off one.
    • Cadric decided not to go after it and called it "the angel's share."
    • Jain then used the corpse arms to hug Cadric.

    Lady Waterford

    • Found a wall filled with DeLawrence Pouchenelle family portraits.
    • One empty spot.
    • Lady Waterford's portrait fit perfectly.
    • Jain put her back with her family.
    • Jain felt the warmth/contentment settle. Lady Waterford finally seemed satisfied.
    • Still don't know where Lady Waterford's body is.

    • Boots of False Tracks

    • Potion of Resistance
    • Scent of the Mists

    Jain identified the potions by tasting them. Of course she did.

    Goodbye, manor

    • Jain set the manor on fire.
    • Left Idlethorp and headed back to Waterford.

    Farrow

    • Returned to collect our reward.
    • We were supposed to retrieve:
    • Lady Waterford's portrait.
    • Lord Waterford's jeweled walking stick.
    • We returned with neither, as far as Farrow knows.
    • Portrait stayed at the DeLawrence Pouchenelle manor.
    • Got 90 gp each as partial payment.
    • Farrow finally told us about the third job:
    • Client wants an object of power.
    • Small enough to fit in a hand.
    • We'd know it when we saw it.
    • We think it might have been the brain.
    • The brain I smashed.
    • Oops.

    Next: Candle Cross

    • Leaving Waterford.
    • Heading toward Mordenshire through Candle Cross.
    • Candle Cross is built in the ruins of an old castle along the Arden River.
    • Its drawbridge supposedly raises and lowers by itself.
    • Some people think it reacts when evil threatens the town.

    Discoveries

    • Glim Brightstone connection: The clockwork ballerina on the fountain in the DeLawrence Pouchenelle manor had a plaque that read, “For my friend of a like mind, happy birthday, Glim Brightstone.” Someone in this family knew Glim personally. We later found a dwarf body in the laboratory. We still don’t know whether that dwarf was Glim, or who Glim’s mysterious assistant was.

    Things we still don't know

    • Where is Lady Waterford's body?
    • Who was the scarred ghost in room 301?
    • Who was the giant stitched man, and who did he mean by "Sister"?
    • What exactly was happening in the DeLawrence Pouchenelle laboratory?
    • Was the dwarf on the lab table Glim Brightstone?
    • Was the brain actually the object Farrow's client wanted?
    • Who is Farrow's client?
    • What happens if Farrow finds out we destroyed the object?
    • What's controlling the drawbridge at Candle Cross?
