    Bia's notes - "It wasn't me" in Mordenshire

    Adventure at a Glance

    Ravenlost finally reached Mordenshire, where we planned to sell our treasure, visit Rudolph Van Richten, and eventually meet Lord Godfroy at the House on Griffin Hill.

    Our visit became considerably more complicated. We helped Van Richten contain a creature released from a mummified cat, learned more about the Mists and Glim Brightstone, discovered the fourth victim of a serial murderer, and agreed to go undercover inside Mordenshire's sanatorium.

    We also finally met Lord Godfroy. He wants us to investigate three locations connected to Glim and find a larger Glim battery. He's offering 15,000 gp each, but we have to leave Mordenshire in one week.

    Ravenlost reached Level 4.

    Arriving in Mordenshire

    Mordenshire is a large, busy coastal town with a harbor.

    We attracted some attention when we arrived. Apparently a halfling, an orc, a Lupine, and a Kalashtar walking into town together is “unusual.”

    The guards told us about three inns:

    • Blackcard - In the center of town. Popular with merchants and conveniently located near a bakery.
    • The Seventh Sea - Near the coast/docks.
    • The Salty Dog - Near the sanatorium and popular with scholars.

    Jain heard "sanatorium" and immediately wanted to stay at the Salty Dog.

    Blackcard

    Bia and Cadric stayed at the Blackcard, Room 304.

    • Innkeeper: Alenta, an elderly Gnome.
    • Alenta had an old grimoire behind the desk.
    • Cadric agreed to perform from 8:00-9:00 p.m.
    • His performance covered our room and board.

    Salty Dog

    Jain and Elisandra stayed at the Salty Dog, Room 213.

    • Innkeeper: Menda.
    • They checked in under the false name "Jainisandra."
    • Bia and Cadric later gave Menda a letter to pass to any guests resembling a Lupine and a Kalashtar.
    • Jain and Elisandra attempted to discreetly leave their second-story room through the window.
    • Bia, Cadric, and everyone else in the bar saw them.

    Very discreet.

    Strange magic

    While we were together at the Salty Dog, we examined some of the magical items we'd collected.

    Elisandra's crown

    Elisandra tried on a magical crown intended for a cleric.

    • The crown requires attunement.
    • When Elisandra put it on, Bia and Jain saw a vague figure appear above her.
    • It looked like a skeletal hand resting on her shoulder.
    • The figure disappeared.

    Jain's stone

    Jain discovered that her mysterious stone is one of a pair of Sending Stones.

    • It can be used once per day.
    • The other stone is held by someone else.
    • Jain tried contacting whoever has the matching stone.
    • No one answered.

    Elisandra also returned Jain's Necklace of Disguise.

    Shopping and selling

    Pyrite's Booty

    We sold our accumulated treasure, including Lord Waterford's jeweled walking stick.

    Total received: 4,000 gp.

    • Bia: 1,000 gp
    • Cadric: 1,100 gp
    • Jain: 1,100 gp
    • Elisandra: 1,100 gp

    Bia's share was smaller because I already had additional gold from the crypt beneath the Waterford mausoleum.

    Hammerbarn

    At the blacksmith:

    • Bia sold her extra greataxe for 27 gp.
    • Jain left her shortsword to be imbued with silver.
    • The process will take one week.
    • The blacksmith can only work on one weapon at a time.
    • Cadric considered leaving his sword but decided against it because his wouldn't be ready until another week after Jain's.

    Atom's Arcana

    Lots of magical things we couldn't afford.

    Cadric found one he could.

    • Eversmoking Bottle
    • Cost: 950 gp

    Rudolph Van Richten

    Jennifer and Lori had recommended that we visit Rudolph Van Richten, a monster hunter and occult expert who operates an herbalist shop in Mordenshire.

    When we arrived, we found his employee Beatrice injured outside.

    • Beatrice had gone inside to open the shop.
    • Something attacked her.
    • Elisandra went inside to investigate.
    • A shadowy black panther attacked Elisandra and knocked her unconscious.
    • After we revived her, Elisandra remained outside with Beatrice.
    • Bia, Cadric, and Jain entered the shop.

    Inside:

    • There was blood on the floor.
    • The shadow panther continued appearing and disappearing.
    • A small, wiry shadow creature threw a flask at Jain and hit her in the back of the head.
    • An unseen force on the stairs tried to stop Jain from going upstairs.
    • Jain got through.
    • Bia followed.
    • Cadric had more trouble, so Bia pulled him up with a rope.

    Upstairs

    Van Richten's living quarters contained research about the different domains of Ravenloft.

    Jain found:

    • Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft
    • a scroll
    • a strange mummified cat covered in markings and broken into two pieces

    Jain tried putting the cat back together.

    Of course she did.

    In the master bedroom, we found Rudolph Van Richten frozen in mid-step. He couldn't move, but his eyes could.

    By asking questions and having him move his eyes left or right, we determined that the scroll Jain found could help him.

    The scroll contained Protection from Evil and Good.

    Cadric read it.

    Van Richten could move again.

    The mummified cat

    Van Richten explained that the mummified cat was responsible for the creatures in the shop.

    We took it to a ritual chamber in his basement.

    • Van Richten placed the mummy inside a magical circle.
    • He had to concentrate on a ritual.
    • Our job was to protect him.
    • Shadows gathered above the mummy and formed the giant black panther.
    • We fought it while Van Richten continued chanting.
    • Cadric was knocked unconscious.
    • The ritual succeeded.
    • The panther dissolved back into the mummy.
    • The two broken pieces of the cat fused together again.

    Afterward, we revived Cadric and had tea.

    What Van Richten told us

    Lord Godfroy

    Van Richten knows quite a bit about Godfroy.

    • Godfroy can control ghosts who died after him.
    • The ghosts apparently have some freedom in how they interpret his orders.
    • Henry Loust was Godfroy's head butler while alive and is probably subject to this control.
    • Lady Estella Godfroy opposes Godfroy.
    • She remains trapped at Griffin Hill after one of Godfroy's experiments killed her.
    • Godfroy's ghosts can move through walls.
    • They can observe people without being seen.

    Something to remember while visiting Griffin Hill.

    Mist Horror

    Jain described what happened to her in the Mists outside Candle Cross.

    Van Richten believes the creature she encountered may have been a Mist Horror.

    • Incorporeal predator.
    • Uses fear to lure victims.
    • Can apparently take forms meaningful to its victims.

    Traveling through the Mists

    Van Richten explained:

    • Talismans associated with specific domains can help travelers reach those domains.
    • A Mist Wanderer can eventually find a route to a named destination.
    • The route may not be safe.
    • The route may not be direct.

    Jain is a Mist Wanderer.

    Glim Brightstone

    We told Van Richten about the strange machine beneath Candle Cross.

    He thought it might be connected to Glim Brightstone and may have contained a Glim battery.

    This sounds similar to the technology we found in the laboratory near the Black Lantern Inn, where we left Sedgwick.

    Van Richten has also heard rumors about the apparatus Godfroy is seeking.

    Depending on which rumors are true, it may:

    • allow someone to travel safely through the Mists;
    • remove a soul from one body and place it into another;
    • possibly do something similar on a much larger scale.

    None of this makes me feel better about Godfroy wanting it.

    Gifts from Van Richten

    Before we left, Van Richten gave us:

    • Lantern of Reveal
    • Stone of Good Luck
    • 6 vials of holy water
    • 4 wooden stakes
    • Tarokka deck

    Van Richten also agreed to make silver canine caps for Jain.

    The breath of the dead

    That evening, Cadric performed at the Blackcard.

    His original song received mild applause.

    The familiar songs went much better.

    Henry Loust was in the audience. Jain noticed him watching us and sat beside him.

    "Jain."

    "Henry."

    Henry told us that Godfroy knew we had reached Mordenshire and expected us to make time for him.

    Meanwhile, a thick fog rolled into town.

    The locals call it:

    The breath of the dead.

    Jain and Elisandra started back toward the Salty Dog.

    Jain noticed a strange flickering light somewhere in the fog.

    Then she smelled blood.

    Elisandra returned to the Blackcard. Bia joined Jain.

    We followed the smell toward the docks.

    Madeline's murder

    We found Madeline, one of the Blackcard's barmaids.

    She was dead.

    • Her throat had been slit.
    • Her tongue had been removed.
    • A chunk of hair had been torn from the back of her head.
    • Blood and skin were beneath her fingernails.
    • She had fought back.
    • The wounds were clean.
    • It appeared the murderer had grabbed her from behind.

    Jain found a trail of blood leading toward the sanatorium.

    Bia went to the Salty Dog looking for help and then ran to the garrison.

    When I returned with guards, Jain was gone.

    Naturally.

    Jain investigates

    Jain followed the blood trail toward the sanatorium.

    A guard told her that he had heard something moving in the bushes.

    Jain's conclusion:

    "Well, fuck, that's the murderer."

    She went into the bushes.

    Two sanatorium guards eventually searched with her.

    They found:

    • footprints leading from pavement into the grass;
    • a bloody dagger approximately six inches long.

    The guards considered that enough and told Jain to return to the docks.

    Jain disagreed.

    "You guys kind of fucking suck, no offense, but this is what I'm good at. Can you let me do this?"

    "You don't work for the city of Mordenshire."

    "Do I need to work for the city to avenge someone's death?"

    Apparently she did.

    "It wasn't me"

    The guards questioned Jain at the docks.

    Her summary:

    "Smell blood, body dark, body dead, blood, blood trail, follow blood trail, find weapon."

    This did not make her look less suspicious.

    The following morning, two guards collected Jain from the Salty Dog and took her to the garrison to speak with Captain Kilm O'Connell.

    Jain explained everything again.

    She also pointed out that she had told the sanatorium guard before going into the bushes.

    "Wouldn't it be more weird if I just ran into the bushes without saying anything?"

    She reminded O'Connell:

    "I played a critical role in this case."

    O'Connell asked:

    "You understand how you look, right?"

    Jain understood.

    "I didn't do it."

    Fortunately, O'Connell already knew that.

    Madeline was the fourth murder victim.

    Jain had only arrived in Mordenshire the previous day.

    "Oh, do you know it wasn't me then?"

    Yes.

    She still looked "suspicious as shit."

    The sanatorium

    Captain O'Connell needed people to investigate Mordenshire's sanatorium.

    The facility operates with very little oversight from the town.

    We later met with:

    • Captain Kilm O'Connell
    • Mayor Alice Heatherman

    The sanatorium is controlled by Dr. Caroline Resdonna.

    • Resdonna brings in outside researchers and staff.
    • The sanatorium largely avoids town oversight.
    • O'Connell wants to know what is happening inside.
    • We also need to determine whether the sanatorium is connected to the murders.

    Our covers

    • Jain - Patient
    • Elisandra - Patient
    • Bia - Janitor
    • Cadric - Groundskeeper

    The operation begins in three days.

    The paperwork for Bia and Cadric should be ready by then.

    The EC Light

    Cadric and Elisandra designed a reusable magical light.

    The EC Light consists of:

    • a copper outer tube;
    • a silver inner component enchanted with Continual Flame;
    • a removable cap that blocks the light when it isn't needed.

    They commissioned two at Hammerbarn:

    • one for Cadric;
    • one for Elisandra.

    Cadric also ordered a custom utility belt from the tannery with:

    • a holster for the EC Light;
    • a special pouch for his Eversmoking Bottle;
    • a design that allows smoke to escape while keeping the bottle secured.

    Elisandra ordered a utility belt with a holster for her EC Light.

    House on Griffin Hill

    We finally went to the House on Griffin Hill.

    The estate is just outside Mordenshire.

    • Tall wrought-iron gates.
    • Gates opened after Bia announced that Henry Loust had invited us.
    • Immaculate grounds.
    • Elaborate hedges and topiaries.
    • Gardeners/workers sometimes appeared at the edge of our vision.
    • When we looked directly at them, no one was there.
    • There was a large area of recently disturbed and compacted earth.

    Henry said they were expanding the manor.

    Inside:

    • The sitting room was strangely dusty compared with the grounds.
    • Cadric found a piano and played it.
    • Elisandra found a leather-bound journal connected to Glim.
    • It was the only journal in the bookcase that wasn't covered in dust.
    • Jain found a decanter containing what appeared to be pure alcohol.
    • Jain heard a metallic scraping/digging noise beneath the floor.
    • Cadric could feel vibrations.

    Something is happening beneath Griffin Hill.

    Lord Godfroy's job

    Godfroy needs "capable mortal hands."

    He wants us to investigate Glim Brightstone and locate a more powerful Glim battery.

    He gave us three leads.

    Welkspring House - Echo Island

    • One of Glim's friends wrote that Welkspring House was a safe haven.
    • Strange blue writing at the house apparently prevents ghosts from entering.

    Cauldron of Tepist

    • Contains fountains associated with resurrection.
    • Godfroy believes they may have inspired part of Glim's apparatus.

    Falkovnia

    We need to find Mikhail Hatsamamous.

    • He established a hospital near a mountain.
    • He helps people suffering from the region's undead affliction.
    • He may know where Glim went.
    • He may know what Glim was working on.

    Payment

    Godfroy wants us to investigate all three leads and find the larger battery.

    Payment:

    15,000 gp per party member.

    We leave in one week.

    Cadric's borrowed sword

    Cadric's ordinary sword won't be useful against ghosts.

    Since he didn't leave it at Hammerbarn to be silvered, Godfroy agreed to lend him a +1 shortsword.

    Henry brought the sword to Cadric.

    Good news:

    It isn't cursed.

    Bad news:

    Godfroy can scry on Cadric through it.
