    Bia's notes - The shadows of Crawford

    Adventure at a glance

    Starting point: Black Lantern Inn Destination: Mordenshire, Mordent Unexpected stop: Crawford Primary mystery: Multiple people and entire families have disappeared without signs of forced entry or robbery. Main suspect/location: Crawford Mano Other suspicious location: Locust Branch Hospital

    Madam Eva's reading

    Madam Eva arrived at the abandoned Black Lantern Inn and performed a card reading.

    First five cards

    Card Reading / Notes
    Mists A journey ahead. The Mists may claim those who aren't careful.
    Miser A lord rules from a mansion. He refuses to give in to something he fears more than death.
    Dictator No interpretation given.
    Beast Cadric pointed at Jain.
    Darklord No interpretation given.

    Cards placed over the first reading

    • Berserker → Dictator
    • Charlatan → Darklord
    • Shepherd → Beast
    • Ghost → Miser

    Cadric believes the cards represent people we already know or will eventually encounter. He’s a smart one.

    Questions

    • Who unlocked the front door when Madam Eva left?
    • Who is the lord represented by the Miser?
    • Does the Ghost placed over the Miser relate to that lord?
    • Who are the Dictator and Darklord?
    • Is Jain actually connected to the Beast card, or was Cadric just being Cadric?

    Traveling through the Mists

    We decided to head north toward Griffin Hill / Mordenshire in Mordent.

    Because visibility was almost nonexistent, we tied ourselves together with rope.

    Impaled body

    We repeatedly encountered a body impaled on a pike.

    Cadric examined it and found a falcon branded into the person's chest.

    Possible significance: Unknown.

    Parry's Potables

    Met Parry, a traveling potion merchant headed toward Dementlieu.

    Notable detail:

    • Wore a jeweled charm shaped like a mask.
    • Confirmed Jain's red vials from the alchemy lab were healing potions.
    • Sold us four additional healing potions.
    • Cadric negotiated a group discount.
    • We also purchased a disguise potion for Jain.

    Cadric considered leaving with Parry.

    He didn't.

    Crawford

    We emerged from the Mists in a marsh approximately a day and a half from Mordenshire.

    Nearby:

    • Crawford
    • Locust Branch Hospital
    • Crawford Mano
    • Pond / river
    • Woods

    Locust Branch Hospital

    Full sign:

    Locust Branch Hospital — Condemned under the Act of Death

    The hospital appears abandoned but is definitely not empty.

    Inside

    Jain entered through a broken window.

    She saw:

    • An abandoned hospital ward.
    • A shiny object beyond swinging doors.
    • A bandaged arm reach through the doors and take the object.

    Elisandra used Augury before we went farther inside.

    Result: Bad idea.

    Bia left.

    Jain eventually did too.

    Lavar

    Lavar is one of the hospital guards.

    He told us:

    • The hospital is dangerous.
    • Crawford has experienced several recent disappearances.
    • Daverick, the night guard Lavar was replacing, was missing.
    • Daverick should have walked to work.
    • There was a town meeting at the Raven's Claw Inn about the disappearances.
    • Crawford is governed by Sterling Crawford.
    • Sterling has been secluded in Crawford Mano since his pregnant wife became ill.

    Pablo

    Lavar's dog.

    Jain talked to him.

    Pablo says there are ghosts around the hospital.

    Specific example:

    • Every night around 11:00 PM, a ghost named Cynthia throws/slams herself into a hospital window.

    Pablo could not smell Daverick along his normal route to work.

    He could smell Daverick near the dock.

    Missing people

    According to Thom Youngblood, Crawford's de facto leader:

    Missing When Notes
    Lone Farmer John 9 days ago Lives closest to Crawford Mano.
    Peterbuild family 5 days ago Husband, wife, and son. Neighbors noticed no smoke from chimney.
    Stern & Persephone Reynolds Last night Husband and wife.
    Daverick Discovered today Hospital night guard; also tended Crawford Mano's grounds.

    Geographic pattern

    Moving outward from Crawford Mano:

    1. Lone Farmer John
    2. Peterbuilds
    3. Reynolds
    4. Lemroys / Darrons nearby

    The disappearances appear to be moving progressively farther away from the manor.

    Crawford Manor

    Sterling Crawford owns/runs the manor and governs Crawford.

    Known information:

    • Sterling's wife became ill during pregnancy.
    • Sterling withdrew into the manor to care for her.
    • The local priest visited when she was approximately 6½ months pregnant.
    • He provided herbs, but they did not help.
    • Sterling then banished him from the manor and decided to care for his wife himself.
    • Mrs. Crawford had previously been pregnant, but the child did not survive.
    • Sterling receives regular shipments from Larder Supply.
    • The most recent shipment disappeared and is now four days overdue.
    • Typical shipments include grain, meat, and linens.
    • Earlier shipments included baby supplies and furniture.
    • Requests for baby items eventually stopped.

    Thema

    Thema is the caretaker at Crawford Mano.

    Known information:

    • Gnome.
    • Approximately 300 years old.
    • Roughly Cadric's size/build.
    • Stark white hair.
    • Apparently rules the manor with an iron fist.

    Daverick's house

    Daverick's house was unlocked.

    Inside:

    • Humble furnishings.
    • Unmade cot.
    • Back door open.
    • Fine porcelain teapot and cup that seemed far too expensive for Daverick.
    • Tea with a strong, astringent smell.
    • Small white flowers in the teapot.

    Outside:

    • Large boot print leading toward/into the river.

    The priest identified the flowers as hemlock.

    Hemlock

    • Paralytic.
    • Native only to Echo Island.
    • Same substance later appears to have been used on Cadric.

    The priest also agreed that the porcelain looked like something the Crawford family could own.

    Reynolds' house

    Evidence:

    • Two sets of footprints near the river.
    • Same size boot.
    • One set of prints was significantly deeper than the other.
    • Table set for three.
    • Two empty cups.
    • No teapot.
    • Tea residue smelled like the tea at Daverick's.
    • Three strands of hair found:
    • Medium brown
    • Long blonde
    • Stark white

    Identifications:

    • Stern Reynolds: medium brown hair
    • Persephone Reynolds: long blonde hair
    • Thema: white hair

    This strongly suggests Thema—or someone with matching white hair—was at the Reynolds' house.

    Question

    Why were the footprints the same size but different depths?

    Was someone carrying another person?

    Peterbuild’s and John’s houses

    Both houses appeared almost untouched.

    Lone Farmer John's was especially clean—possibly too clean for such a humble home.

    No:

    • Teapot
    • Cups
    • Obvious tea residue
    • Other obvious evidence

    Question

    Were these houses cleaned after the victims disappeared?

    The pond

    Jain and Bia investigated the pond before nightfall.

    Jain's discovery

    Near where the river current enters the pond, Jain searched the silt and found:

    • Human skull
    • Deep, uneven scrapes on the bone
    • Scrapes did not appear to have been made by metal

    Unknown whether the damage occurred before or after death.

    Bia's discovery

    Bia dove into the middle of the pond and recovered a burlap sack weighted down with a rock.

    Inside were three additional sacks.

    Each contained infant bones.

    Major questions

    • Who were the infants?
    • Are they connected to Mrs. Crawford?
    • Why were their remains dumped in the pond?
    • What caused the damage to the adult skull?
    • How long have the remains been there?

    Watching the Lemroy house

    Earnest and Ella Lemroy agreed to stay at the inn while we watched their property.

    The perfect plan

    Because the Lemroys are halflings:

    • Cadric: Disguise himself as Mr. Lemroy and serve as "bait."
    • Jain: Hide under the bed.
    • Elisandra: Watch from the barn and telepathically warn Cadric.
    • Bia: Watch from the barn roof.

    It was the perfect plan.

    The hooded figure

    At approximately 11:30 PM, Bia saw a roughly four-foot-tall hooded figure emerge from the woods.

    It carried:

    • Kettle
    • Pouch
    • Cup

    The figure knocked on the Lemroy door.

    Cadric answered.

    It offered him a cup and said:

    "Drink."

    Cadric was magically compelled to obey.

    The tea:

    • Tasted vile.
    • Paralyzed him.
    • Appeared to contain hemlock.

    The figure entered and whistled.

    Attack

    Bia swung at the figure.

    Her axe passed through the cloak.

    There was nothing inside it.

    The figure disappeared, leaving behind:

    • Kettle containing the remaining tea
    • Lantern

    Elisandra heard:

    • Something nearby.
    • Water moving toward the creek/manor.

    Jain saw:

    • A black figure flying toward Crawford Mano.

    Important

    The attacker apparently:

    1. Approaches a home.
    2. Offers poisoned tea.
    3. Compels the victim to drink.
    4. Paralyzes the victim.
    5. Summons or signals something.
    6. Somehow transports the victim toward the river/manor.

    This may explain the missing families.

    Cadric vs. hemlock

    Cadric remained paralyzed for approximately 15 minutes.

    Unfortunately for Elisandra, paralysis did not prevent telepathy.

    She briefly considered moving more than twenty feet away so she would no longer have to listen to his complaints and expletives.

    Bia eventually put him on the bed.

    Apparently, he asked.

    Shadow attack

    During Cadric's watch at approximately 1:30 AM, the room began growing darker.

    He turned toward the fire.

    There were three shadow figures around it.

    Cadric screamed.

    Battle followed.

    Result:

    • 1 shadow killed.
    • 2 shadows fled.

    No further attacks that night.

    Questions

    Were the shadows connected to:

    • Crawford Mano?
    • The hooded figure?
    • Locust Branch Hospital?
    • Something else entirely?

    What we know before entering Crawford Mano

    Evidence connecting the disappearances to the manor:

    1. Missing households are progressively farther from Crawford Mano.
    2. Daverick also worked on the manor grounds.
    3. Daverick had an expensive porcelain tea service likely associated with the Crawfords.
    4. Daverick's tea contained hemlock.
    5. Reynolds' cups contained similar residue.
    6. White hair found at the Reynolds' house matches Thema.
    7. A hooded figure attempted the same poisoned-tea method at the Lemroy house.
    8. The hooded figure was approximately Thema's size.
    9. After disappearing, something traveled through the water toward the manor.
    10. Jain saw a black figure fly toward Crawford Mano.
    11. Human and infant remains were found in the nearby pond.
    12. Sterling Crawford has isolated himself since his wife's illness and pregnancy.
    13. The Crawfords previously lost another pregnancy.
    14. Baby supplies to the manor abruptly stopped.
    15. The manor's most recent supply shipment has disappeared.

    Current objective

    Thom gave us a rough map of the Crawford Mano grounds and the portion of the house he remembers from childhood.

    The townspeople now agree that something nefarious is happening at the manor.

    Next stop:

    Crawford Mano.
