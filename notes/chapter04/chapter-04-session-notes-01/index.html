    Bia's notes - Worst-laid plans

    Adventure at a glance

    Item Details
    Location Mordenshire Sanatorium
    Goal Investigate the sanatorium and determine whether it is connected to the murders in Mordenshire
    Party covers Bia and Jain — patients; Cadric — groundskeeper; Elisandra — janitor "Sandra Korn"
    Key NPCs Dr. Caroline Resdonna, Dr. Dave, Dr. Van Larder, Peter, Helen, Cookie, Belinda
    Patients Randolph, Janice, Robert, Wendel, Trashcan
    Major discoveries Wendel disappeared into the ICU; strange writing hidden throughout his old room; dissected brain and "GE" clue in the morgue; third floor is restricted; orderlies obey Dr. Caroline's voice
    Current situation Dr. Caroline poisoned and injured; Jain loose in the ICU; Cadric disguised as Dr. Caroline; party still unarmed inside the sanatorium
    Cliffhanger Dr. Dave cast Fireball. I failed my save and took 18 damage.

    Ready to roll

    We returned to the mayor's residence after meeting with Lord Godfroy and agreed to investigate the sanatorium.

    The mayor already had employee badges for Cadric and Elisandra. The plan:

    • Cadric: Groundskeeper
    • Elisandra: Janitor, using the name Sandra Korn
    • Jain: Patient, after causing a disturbance at the docks
    • Bia: Patient, after warning everyone in town about an approaching undead army

    We left our weapons and armor at the garrison before going in the next day.

    We had one goal. Investigate what was happening at the sanatorium.

    What could go wrong?

    The sanatorium

    The sanatorium has three floors and a basement.

    First floor

    • Intake area
    • Lower-security patients
    • Nurses' station
    • Common areas
    • Kitchen
    • Staff areas
    • Access to the grounds/farm
    • I was initially placed here as a lower-security patient.

    Second floor

    • Intake area
    • Higher-security patients
    • Jain was placed in a cell here.
    • ICU
    • Nurses' station
    • Lobby with a large window overlooking Mordenshire
    • Dr. Caroline has access to the ICU.
    • Dr. Dave also works in the ICU.
    • Dr. Van Larder does not work in the ICU.
    • I was allowed on this floor to "watch" for the undead army.

    Third floor

    • Off limits.
    • Cadric reached the locked gate but didn't get beyond it.
    • We still don't know what's up there.

    Basement

    • Boiler
    • Morgue
    • Janitor's quarters
    • Elisandra is responsible for tending the boiler several times a day.
    • The boiler helps power the elevator.
    • Helen is her boss.

    People

    Dr. Caroline Resdonna

    Currently in charge of the sanatorium.

    • Took over the sanatorium with Dr. Dave approximately 18 months ago.
    • Remodeled part of the former high-security wing into the ICU.
    • Has her quarters on the 3rd floor.
    • Has access to the ICU.
    • Eats separately from the other staff.
    • Questioned Jain inside the ICU.
    • Cadric successfully drugged her stew with hemlock.
    • Jain attacked and choked her after escaping her restraints.
    • The orderlies respond to her commands.

    Dr. Dave

    Arrived with Dr. Caroline approximately 18 months ago.

    • Dr. Van Larder said Dr. Dave and Dr. Caroline are old friends.
    • Works in/accesses the ICU.
    • Spoke with me about the "undead army" and allowed me to work outside on defenses.
    • Was involved when Jain was taken to the ICU.
    • Appeared from deeper inside the ICU during the confrontation with Dr. Caroline.
    • Cast Fireball at us at the end of the session.

    Dr. Van Larder

    Elderly doctor who has worked at the sanatorium for 43 years.

    • Doesn't work in or have access to the ICU.
    • Told me that only Dr. Caroline and Dr. Dave go into the ICU.
    • Seemed strangely unconcerned by the condition of Wendel's old room.

    Peter

    Night watchman.

    • Cadric's boss.
    • Showed Cadric his quarters and explained his groundskeeping duties.

    Helen

    Nighttime janitor.

    • Trained Elisandra on her duties.
    • Told Elisandra that the ICU should only be cleaned when specifically requested.

    Cookie

    Sanatorium cook.

    • Cadric befriended him.
    • Asked Cadric to get butter from town.
    • Was making molasses cookies.
    • Helped Cadric learn Dr. Caroline's eating habits.
    • Cadric used his kitchen access to drug Dr. Caroline's stew.
    • Cadric planned to also drug 4 cookies and deliver those to nurses and other nighttime staff.

    Belinda

    Intake nurse.

    • Handled Jain's intake.
    • Put Jain in "Wendel's old room."

    Randolph

    Lower-security patient.

    • Has been at the sanatorium for about two years.
    • From Mordent.

    Janice

    Lower-security patient.

    • Has been at the sanatorium for about three years.
    • From Mordent.
    • Told me about the ICU and the changes Dr. Caroline made to the sanatorium.

    Robert

    Lower-security patient.

    • Has been at the sanatorium for about six months.
    • Originally from Barovia.
    • Says he became lost in the Mists and eventually arrived here.

    Wendel

    Previous occupant of Jain's room.

    • Was taken to the ICU approximately one week ago.
    • Hasn't returned.
    • His room was much more damaged than the other patient rooms.
    • Writing was hidden beneath the padding, floorboards, and other surfaces.

    Trashcan

    • A patient who lives inside a trashcan and has apparently been at the sanatorium for more than two years.
    • Jain discovered that she could communicate with him by asking yes-or-no questions:
    • Trash = yes
    • Can = no
    • Trashcan confirmed that he had been at the sanatorium for more than two years and that he collects trash.
    • Jain told him that she wanted to escape and asked if he could help her get out. Trashcan shuffled closer to her cell. After Jain unsuccessfully tried to pick her lock without tools, Trashcan said, "Trash," reached inside his trashcan, and produced a lock. It wasn't particularly useful, but he appeared to understand what Jain wanted and attempted to help.
    • Dr. Dave later told Sandra that the staff have been trying for years to get Trashcan out of the trashcan, but that he becomes "a different being" when outside it.
    • Still unclear: What exactly Trashcan is, why he refuses to leave the trashcan, and how freely he can move around the sanatorium.

    The orderlies

    The orderlies are enormous bald men, some nearly as large as me.

    Things we noticed:

    • Rarely or never speak.
    • Show almost no emotional response.
    • Extremely strong.
    • Follow instructions immediately.
    • Seem to respond to Dr. Caroline's voice.
    • Jain successfully imitated Dr. Caroline and ordered them to stop.
    • They obeyed her.

    Important: They may be responding to Dr. Caroline's voice, not necessarily Dr. Caroline herself.

    Wendel's room

    Jain was placed in Wendel's old room.

    It was noticeably more damaged than the other rooms. Jain began pulling away padding, floorboards, and pieces of the walls.

    The same two messages appeared repeatedly:

    "She will come visit you."

    "No, he won't."

    Some of the writing appeared to have been made with blood and other substances.

    We don't know who she or he refers to.

    The ICU

    Other patients warned me:

    "You generally don't come back."

    Wendel was taken there a week ago and hasn't returned.

    I briefly saw through the ICU door when Dr. Caroline entered. The first room looked very different from the rest of the sanatorium:

    • Oak paneling
    • Leather chaise
    • Chair beside it
    • Looked more like a therapist's office than a hospital room
    • Another door led farther into the ICU
    • Jain was eventually taken there and restrained on the chaise.

    Dr. Caroline questioned her while eating dinner.

    Dr. Dave later emerged from a room farther inside the ICU.

    We still don't know what's behind the second door.

    The morgue

    Elisandra discovered the recently autopsied body of a woman in the basement morgue.

    • Skull had been opened.
    • Brain had been cut into sections.
    • An anatomical drawing of a brain was nearby.
    • Part of the left hemisphere was marked: GE

    We don't know what GE means or what was done to the woman's brain.

    Talking about "gardening"

    I managed to get outside by convincing Dr. Dave that I needed to prepare defenses against the undead army.

    I was put to work on the grounds.

    Cadric and I then spoke to each other in Halfling, pretending to discuss gardening and farming techniques so the orderlies and other patients wouldn't understand us.

    This gave Cadric a way to update me on what everyone had discovered and his plan for that night.

    Cadric's plan

    Cadric left the sanatorium after his shift to get butter for Cookie.

    He also stopped at the garrison and retrieved his remaining hemlock.

    He successfully smuggled it back into the sanatorium.

    The plan was to:

    1. Drug Dr. Caroline's dinner.
    2. Prepare three drugged molasses cookies for other staff.
    3. Wait until later that night.
    4. Investigate the restricted areas while Dr. Caroline was incapacitated.
    5. Help Jain and me get out as needed.

    Cadric successfully added hemlock to Dr. Caroline's stew.

    The cookies never happened.

    Jain had other plans

    Jain woke inside the ICU restrained while Dr. Caroline ate the poisoned stew.

    Dr. Caroline questioned her, but Jain refused to answer.

    Eventually, Dr. Caroline passed out.

    Jain:

    • Dislocated her thumb.
    • Escaped her restraints.
    • Attacked Dr. Caroline when she regained consciousness.
    • Choked Dr. Caroline.
    • Caused Dr. Caroline to scream before the nighttime plan was ready.

    Because it wasn't lights-out yet, I was still allowed in the upstairs lobby.

    Cadric, Elisandra, and I converged on the second floor after hearing Dr. Caroline scream.

    Two Dr. Carolines

    As the orderlies approached, Jain imitated Dr. Caroline's voice and ordered them to stop.

    It worked.

    Cadric then:

    1. Drank his Potion of Growth.
    2. Grew to approximately Dr. Caroline's height.
    3. Cast Disguise Self to look like Dr. Caroline.
    4. Broke into the ICU.

    There were now two Dr. Carolines:

    • The real Dr. Caroline, being choked by Jain.
    • Cadric, disguised as Dr. Caroline.

    Dr. Dave emerged from deeper inside the ICU and saw the situation.

    He raised his hand.

    A small orange mote, about the size of a marble, appeared and streaked toward the doorway.

    Fireball.

    That's where we stopped.

    Things we still need to figure out

    • What's behind the second ICU door?
    • What's on the third floor?
    • What happened to Wendel?
    • Who are "she" and "he" in the messages in Wendel's room?
    • What does GE mean?
    • Why was the dead woman's brain dissected?
    • Who was the woman in the morgue?
    • What are the orderlies?
    • Why do the orderlies obey Dr. Caroline's voice?
    • Can someone other than Jain imitate Dr. Caroline and control them?
    • What exactly are Dr. Caroline and Dr. Dave doing?
    • Why doesn't Dr. Van Larder ever enter the ICU?
    • How much does Dr. Van Larder know?
    • Why did Dr. Caroline go toward Peter's house?
    • What is Trashcan's deal?
    • What happened to the other patients who went into the ICU?

    Items / money

    • Sent two letters home.
    • Sent 1 platinum piece home.
    • Cost to send the two letters: 4 gp
    • Weapons and armor are currently at the garrison.
    • Cadric retrieved the remaining hemlock from the garrison.
    • Cadric used his Potion of Growth.
    • Dr. Caroline consumed the hemlock in her stew.

    Important for next time

    We are unarmed.

    Our weapons and armor are still at the garrison.

    I failed my save and took 18 damage.

    Dr. Caroline is injured and is being held/choked by Jain.

    Cadric is still disguised as Dr. Caroline.

    Jain had demonstrated that the orderlies would obey her imitation of Dr. Caroline's voice.

    Dr. Dave is present.

    We're still inside the sanatorium.

    This could be going better.

    Bia's journal

    Pretending an undead army was coming seemed like a good idea at the time.

    It got me into the sanatorium. It got me upstairs. It even got me outside, after I convinced a doctor that I needed to help defend Mordenshire. I was then allowed to discuss "gardening" with Cadric.

    So really, that part of the plan worked perfectly.

    Cadric had a plan too. His involved hemlock, stew, cookies, and waiting until everyone was asleep.

    Jain's plan was different.

    Now Dr. Caroline has been poisoned, Jain is choking her, Cadric looks like Dr. Caroline, the giant bald orderlies will apparently listen to anyone who sounds like Dr. Caroline, and Dr. Dave just threw a fireball at us.

    I miss the undead army.
