    Bia's notes - Through the Mists

    Adventure at a glance

    Ravenlost escaped Glim Brightstone's silver mine, fought the creatures guarding Klem's remains, and fulfilled its promise to carry the ghost miner's bones outside. Klem vanished afterward, although whether he found peace is unknown.

    The party then entered the Mists, found an eleven-year-old refugee named Matteo, and learned more about the undead invasion of Falkovnia. Along the way they crossed a ravine above a monstrous mass of bodies, heard a battle involving a wagon but chose not to investigate, and encountered five burned deserters impaled on stakes.

    Near the Stone Spine Mountains, Ravenlost met Zlava, an elderly woman imprisoned in a watchtower for stealing bread. She shared information about the undead hordes and warned the party not to trust Commander Korath. After resting and reaching level 5, Ravenlost freed Zlava and set out toward Delmunster with Matteo.

    Current location: Near the Stone Spine Mountains in Falkovnia, heading toward Delmunster through the Forest of Strangers.

    Party level: 5.

    Traveling companion: Matteo. Zlava departed separately into the Mists.

    Escaping Glim Brightstone's silver mine

    The climb

    • Ravenlost began inside Glim's vault.
    • With the elevator broken, the party climbed approximately 65 feet to the upper mine using rope.
    • Cadric spent part of the ascent partially inside Elisandra's bag of holding.
    • Ravenlost still intended to recover Klem's remains before leaving.

    The web-covered chamber

    The chamber was thickly coated in webs, almost like the inside of a cocoon. The strands looked silvery and wet in the light.

    • Klem had been persuaded to scout ahead. A giant spider tried to attack him, but the attack passed through his ghostly form, and he retreated.
    • Elisandra threw illuminated stones into the chamber.
    • Cadric investigated and saw a spider-like humanoid examining the light.
    • Bia attempted to sneak in but was unsuccessful.
    • Bia attempted to communicate with one of the smaller creatures using Beast Sense; the attempt failed because it was not an animal.
    • Jain tried to distract the creatures with a performance, without success.
    • Jain used her Necklace of Disguise to resemble one of the spider-like humanoids.

    The battle

    Creature Confirmed outcome
    Giant spider 1 Killed
    Giant spider 2 Killed
    Spider-like humanoid 1 Badly injured; escaped back through the tunnel
    Spider-like humanoid 2 Badly injured; climbed out through the Honda-sized opening overhead
    • Jain attempted to shove a spider into a web; it caught itself on a strand.
    • Jain used a howl that hindered enemies.
    • Elisandra cast a fire Chromatic Orb.
    • Ravenlost let the two injured humanoids flee instead of pursuing them.

    Suspected, not confirmed: The creature that fled through the tunnel may have escaped into the woods. Ravenlost did not encounter it again.

    Unanswered: What were the spider-like humanoids? Could either return?

    Klem, the ghost of the mine

    Recovering his remains

    Bia moved the boulders covering Klem's crushed remains. Much of the skeleton was reduced to dust, although his legs were still recognizable.

    Bia told Cadric to fetch Klem and let him know the chamber was safe.

    The party also recovered a silver dowsing rod near the remains. Klem said he had used it to find minerals, chiefly silver and sometimes salt.

    Keeping the promise

    • The dowsing rod went into Elisandra's bag of holding.
    • Ravenlost placed Klem's remains in a minecart and brought them outside.
    • Klem said, “That should work.”
    • He vanished.

    Confirmed: Ravenlost recovered Klem's remains and brought them outside the mine.

    Unanswered: Did Klem's disappearance mean he was finally freed? The party did not confirm his fate.

    Journey into the Mists

    Ravenlost tied themselves together with rope to avoid becoming separated.

    • Visibility was approximately 20 feet.
    • The temperature dropped by roughly 10°F.
    • The surroundings shifted into a sparse, windswept landscape.
    • After approximately eight hours, Jain spotted a dilapidated barn.

    The abandoned barn

    An Augury warned against staying at the barn, but the party investigated.

    They found a dead cow, apparently six to eight weeks old, with bite marks. Small footprints and movement in the hayloft suggested someone was there.

    Finding Matteo

    The person hiding in the hayloft was Matteo, an eleven-year-old blond boy from Aerie, in southern Falkovnia. Ravenlost gave him food and water and agreed to bring him along.

    Detail What Matteo reported
    Age 11
    Hometown Aerie, southern Falkovnia
    Time hiding Approximately nine days
    Parents Missing following an undead attack
    Sisters Susie and Haley, seven-year-old twins; also missing
    Destination Traveling with Ravenlost; hoping to find safety

    Unanswered: What happened to Matteo's parents and sisters? His account establishes that they were separated, not that they died.

    What Matteo reported about Falkovnia

    The invasion

    Matteo described zombies, undead ogres, undead birds, and other horrors. Refugees were being directed toward Lekar, particularly the Red Gate. Ravenlost also encountered undead birds that fled into the Mists.

    Commander Korath and the Talons

    Matteo described Commander Korath, a woman, as a harsh ruler who governs with an iron fist.

    • The Talons enforce military authority.
    • Conscription can begin at fourteen, though farmers may sometimes be exempt.
    • Desertion can carry severe punishment.

    Reported, not independently verified: Matteo's descriptions of Korath's government and the military's rules.

    Why this matters to Bia

    Bia left Falkovnia approximately six weeks earlier, shortly before the invasion began. Her adoptive father, Tobin Underfoot, and adoptive sister, Mira, were still there when she left.

    Matteo suggested survivors might have fled toward Lekar.

    Possible lead: Tobin and Mira may have sought refuge in Lekar.

    Not known: Whether they survived or reached the city.

    The ravine and the moving mass of bodies

    Ravenlost reached a ravine approximately 30 feet wide. Far below, an enormous mass of compressed bodies moved through the valley; individual bodies sometimes broke away and fell.

    Crossing the ravine

    • Elisandra, Cadric, and Matteo climbed into the bag of holding.
    • Bia carried the bag.
    • Jain cast Jump on Bia.
    • Bia and Jain jumped across in sync, and everyone crossed safely.

    Matteo said the creature resembled something he had seen near his family's farm.

    Unanswered: What was the creature, and what caused or directed it?

    The wagon on the hill

    After crossing the ravine, Ravenlost heard sounds of fighting from a hillside. Someone shouted:

    “Circle up and protect the wagon!”

    The party considered investigating, including the possibility of finding useful items. But everyone was exhausted, the Mists remained dangerous, and another fight was not appealing.

    Decision: Ravenlost kept walking. They did not investigate the wagon, join the fight, or collect any loot—a surprisingly restrained choice for the party.

    Unanswered: Who was fighting, what was in the wagon, and what happened afterward?

    The price of desertion

    Later, Ravenlost found five burned bodies impaled on stakes beside the road.

    Matteo identified them as Talon deserters. The bodies were visible evidence of brutality, although the party did not independently investigate who had executed them.

    The party traveled for approximately 40 hours through the Mists in total, accumulating exhaustion. Bia carried Matteo during part of the journey.

    At sunrise, Ravenlost emerged near the Stone Spine Mountains.

    Zlava and the watchtower

    The woman in the tower

    Ravenlost approached a watchtower approximately 60 feet tall. An elderly woman named Zlava was confined there.

    Detail Zlava's account
    Why she was imprisoned Stealing bread
    Time in tower Approximately a week and a half
    Assigned duty Watch for undead hordes and light a warning brazier
    Supplies Limited and dwindling
    Status at session end Freed; departed into the Mists alone

    The undead hordes

    Zlava reported that:

    • The hordes began appearing six or seven weeks earlier.
    • Waves sometimes arrived at intervals of roughly two weeks.
    • Different hordes traveled in different directions.
    • The Talons had suffered heavy losses.
    • Some rumors placed a horde's size at 10,000 undead, but that figure was unverified.
    • Survivors burned remains after attacks to prevent the dead from rising again.

    Unanswered: Are the hordes moving according to a plan? Who or what could be directing them?

    Resting near the watchtower

    Watch order

    Watch Events
    Jain Matteo woke briefly
    Cadric No specific incident established in the available summary
    Elisandra Zlava noticed movement in the trees; Elisandra communicated with her telepathically
    Bia Bia talked with Matteo about the party, her family, and Falkovnia

    During Elisandra's watch, Zlava noticed movement in the trees. Roughly 40 minutes later, she indicated the area was clear. No creature was identified and no fight occurred.

    Bia's conversation with Matteo

    • Cadric: Bia described him as a good person, a talented entertainer, and brave when needed. Matteo thought Cadric was “a lot.”
    • Tobin and Mira: Bia explained that her adoptive family remained in Falkovnia when she left.
    • Lekar: Matteo suggested survivors might have fled there, but had no direct information about Bia's family.
    • Undead attacks: Matteo discussed the scale of the invasion and the burning of remains afterward.
    • Midnight wisps: Bia asked whether Matteo had seen strange wisps around midnight. He had not.

    Important distinction: Matteo offered Bia a possible destination for her search, not confirmation that Tobin and Mira were safe.

    Reaching level 5

    After a long rest, Ravenlost recovered from one level of exhaustion and reached level 5.

    Bia's attribute Level 5
    Maximum hit points 73 HP
    Proficiency bonus +3
    Walking speed 40 feet
    Extra Attack Two attacks when taking the Attack action

    Reminder: Extra Attack is part of the Attack action, not an additional bonus action.

    Freeing Zlava

    Ravenlost decided not to leave Zlava imprisoned for stealing bread.

    • The party used rope to reach the upper part of the tower.
    • Bia climbed up and helped free Zlava.
    • Zlava gathered the belongings and supplies she could carry.
    • The party recovered its rope.

    Zlava offered two warnings:

    “Don't fight. Run.”

    “Don't trust the commander.”

    Confirmed: Zlava warned Ravenlost against trusting Commander Korath.

    Unanswered: What experiences or information lay behind that warning?

    Zlava left alone into the Mists. Matteo stayed with Ravenlost.

    The road to Delmunster

    Location What Ravenlost learned
    Delmunster Closest settlement; approximately 12 hours away
    Forest of Strangers Route to Delmunster
    Lekar / Red Gate Refugee destination; approximately four to five days away
    Stone Spine Mountains Region where Ravenlost emerged from the Mists

    Zlava said the Forest of Strangers had been relatively untouched when she last traveled through it, about a week earlier. This was a report, not a guarantee of safety.

    End of Session 11: Ravenlost headed toward Delmunster with Matteo. Zlava went her own way.

    Commander Korath — facts, reports, and suspicions

    Event or source What Bia knows or has heard Status
    Childhood village attack Bia heard the name Korath; her father Voros died and her mother Anthea disappeared Bia's memory of the attack
    Later farm attack Bia heard Korath again, but cannot remember the fight itself Bia's memory and missing memory
    Ala'kir Identified Commander Korath as a military leader defending a Falkovnian stronghold Reported by Ala'kir
    Matteo Described Korath's harsh rule and the Talons' conscription practices Reported by Matteo
    Five impaled bodies Matteo identified them as Talon deserters Bodies witnessed; identification reported
    Zlava Warned Ravenlost not to trust the commander Warning heard directly

    What is established

    • Commander Korath is female.
    • Bia has encountered the name Korath in three distinct periods of her life.
    • Matteo and Zlava gave the party reasons to be wary of Korath's rule.

    What remains a theory

    • The commander might be the same Korath whose name Bia heard during her childhood and the farm attack.
    • Korath might know something about Anthea's disappearance or Bia's missing memories.

    No connection between the commander and either earlier attack has been proven.

    Next: Reach Delmunster, keep Matteo safe, and seek news of refugees who may know what happened to Tobin and Mira. Bia must also decide how to approach Commander Korath without mistaking suspicion for proof.
